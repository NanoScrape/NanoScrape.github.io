Option 1: three steps

STEP 1

Open Job Feed and sign in

Job Feed runs on Apify. Click the button, then Try for free. Signing up is free; you can use your Google account.

Open Job Feed on Apify
STEP 2

Paste your search

Pick the example closest to you and click Copy. In Apify, switch the input from Manual to JSON, select everything and paste. Then change the job title and town to yours.

{
  "countries": ["UK"],
  "keywords": ["Nurse"],
  "location": "Manchester",
  "radiusKm": 20,
  "postedWithinDays": 7,
  "maxResultsPerPlatform": 20,
  "maxResultsTotal": 100
}
{
  "countries": ["US"],
  "keywords": ["Customer Service"],
  "location": "Austin",
  "radiusKm": 30,
  "postedWithinDays": 7,
  "maxResultsPerPlatform": 20,
  "maxResultsTotal": 100
}
{
  "countries": ["UK"],
  "keywords": ["Marketing"],
  "location": "London",
  "postedWithinDays": 7,
  "companyUrls": [
    "https://boards.greenhouse.io/deliveroo"
  ],
  "maxResultsPerPlatform": 20,
  "maxResultsTotal": 100
}
  • keywords: the job titles you want. You can list several: ["Nurse", "Healthcare Assistant"].
  • location and radiusKm: your town and how far you will travel, in km (20 km is about 12 miles).
  • postedWithinDays: only jobs from the last 7 days.
  • companyUrls: the careers pages of companies you would like to work for. Many of their jobs never reach the big job sites. The example has one company's careers page; swap in the companies you want. Each company costs a bit more than a job site search, so add the ones you really want.
  • maxResultsPerPlatform and maxResultsTotal: at most 20 jobs from each site and 100 jobs in total, so your first search stays small. (Sites that already started searching still finish and count their own results.)
STEP 3

Press Start and open your list

Press Save & Start. When the run finishes, open the Output tab: one row per job, with the title, company, place, date and an apply link to the original ad. Click Export to download it for Excel or Google Sheets.

Want new jobs every morning? Save the search as a task, add a schedule (for example daily at 7:00), set "postedWithinDays": 2 and "onlyNewJobs": true. Each run then brings only jobs you have not seen yet. The first run brings everything, and job sites still count repeats they return, so the short date window is what keeps daily runs small.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You fill in a form (or paste one of the examples above) and press Start. The Claude option works in plain language.

What does it cost?

Signing up on Apify is free. Each search uses credits from your Apify account, and the Job Feed pricing tab shows the current price before you start. Keep the job limit low for your first search.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No. It finds jobs and gives you the links. You read them and apply on the company's or job site's own page.

Can I get new jobs every day?

Yes. On Apify, add a schedule to your saved search, set Posted within to 1 or 2 days and turn on Only new jobs, so each run brings only jobs you have not seen yet.

Which countries and job sites?

Job Feed covers 130+ job sites: national job sites in 38 countries, including the UK and the US, plus Indeed and LinkedIn (and Glassdoor when you search a whole country rather than a town). The Job Feed page lists them all.
