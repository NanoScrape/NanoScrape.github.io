Job API: query jobs like an API

Every NanoScrape job scraper runs on Apify, so every one of them is also an API. You send keywords, a country and a location; you get back structured job rows. The same call works from curl, Python, JavaScript, n8n, Make or an AI agent over MCP. Job board APIs, where they exist at all, are usually partner-only or limited to the board's own ads. Here one input schema covers every source.

Start a run and wait for the results in one request:

curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/santamaria-automations~job-feed/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"keywords": ["data engineer"], "countries": ["DE"], "location": "Berlin", "postedWithinDays": 7, "maxResultsTotal": 200}'

The same from Python:

from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN")
run = client.actor("santamaria-automations/job-feed").call(run_input={
    "keywords": ["data engineer"],
    "countries": ["DE"],
    "location": "Berlin",
    "postedWithinDays": 7,
    "maxResultsTotal": 200,
})
for job in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    print(job["title"], job["company"], job["location"])

Main input fields:

FieldWhat it does
keywordsSearch terms, sent to every selected board. Results are deduplicated across keywords and boards.
countriesCountry markets. Each one switches on its main boards.
platformsName boards explicitly, on top of the country defaults.
location, radiusKmCity or region plus a radius. Rows clearly outside the radius are dropped before they are charged.
postedWithinDaysOnly recent ads. 1 = last 24 hours.
careerSites, companyUrlsCompany career pages to read alongside the boards.
onlyNewJobsDaily runs return only jobs you have not received before.

Each row has the same core fields, whatever board it came from: title, company, location, salary (when the ad states it), posted_at, apply_url and the source board. The Job Feed page lists every output field.

Recruitment data use cases

  • Agency lead generation: companies hiring right now for roles you place are your warmest leads. Search by role and region, then group the rows by company. See finding companies that are hiring in Germany.
  • Competitor hiring: new roles at a competitor show where it is investing. Monitor competitor career pages on a schedule and get only the new ads.
  • Sourcing and job boards of your own: fill a niche job board or an internal job wall with fresh, deduplicated ads.
  • Sales signals: a company hiring five data engineers probably needs data tools. Filter by title and turn the companies into a target list.

Job market and labor market data

Run the same search every day and the rows become a time series: how many ads per role, city and company, how fast new ones appear, which salaries are stated. Every row carries its posting date and source, so counts can be rebuilt and checked. Our Job Market Index is built this way; the Switzerland edition shows what the data supports.

Two limits to keep in mind. Salary is only there when the ad states it, which in many countries is a minority of ads. And job ads measure demand that employers advertise, not all hiring, so treat them as a leading indicator, not as official labor statistics.

Automate it: n8n and no-code

Job Feed runs on a schedule in Apify, or from n8n, Make and Zapier through the Apify integrations. A daily run with onlyNewJobs on returns just the ads you have not seen, ready for a sheet, a CRM or a Slack channel. The guide to scraping every job online walks through the setup.

Pricing

You pay per job on Apify, with no subscription. A Job Feed run costs the price of each board scraper it starts (listed on that scraper's page) plus Job Feed's own fee of $0.50 per 1,000 unique jobs it delivers. Duplicates removed across boards are not charged the Job Feed fee. Single-board scrapers charge only their own price. Apify's free plan includes $5 of monthly credit, enough to test before you pay anything.

FAQ

Is scraping job boards legal?

Job ads are published so that anyone can read them, and our scrapers only collect what a visitor sees without logging in. The rules still depend on your country, the site's terms and what you do with the data, especially any personal data such as recruiter names. This is general information, not legal advice; check with a lawyer for your use case.

How fresh is the data?

Each run reads the boards live, so the data is as fresh as the boards at run time. Use postedWithinDays to keep only recent ads and a daily schedule with onlyNewJobs to receive new ads only.

How are duplicates handled?

Job Feed matches the same job across boards and keywords and keeps one row. The other boards it appeared on are listed in the run's sources manifest.

Which countries are covered?

Job Feed covers national job boards in 38 countries and the Middle East, plus 11 international and 10 remote job boards. Coverage is deepest in Switzerland, Germany and Austria. The board table above lists every board by country.

Is there an API rate limit?

Runs use Apify's API, so Apify's account limits apply, not per-board limits. Job Feed spreads the work over the boards and caps how many run in parallel, so one slow board does not hold up the rest.

Can I export to Google Sheets, CSV or Excel?

Yes. Every run's results download as JSON, CSV or Excel from Apify, and the Google Sheets, n8n, Make and Zapier integrations push them on to wherever you need them.
