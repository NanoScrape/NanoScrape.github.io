What you get

  • REST API: GET /v1/jobs?company=nvidia&updated_since=2026-08-25
  • Webhooks: subscribe to new / removed / updated jobs per company or per keyword
  • Unified schema: same field names regardless of underlying ATS (Workday, Phenom, iCIMS, or custom LLM extraction)
  • Freshness SLA: F500 and Global 2000 refreshed every 24 hours
  • Salary parsing: numeric ranges extracted where publicly disclosed
  • ATS analytics: query hiring velocity by industry, seniority, geography, and remote status without running any scrapers yourself

Under the hood

JobFeed orchestrates our three-actor extraction stack:

  • Tier 1 — Career Site Jobs Scraper: hits 24 ATS platforms directly via their public JSON APIs (Workday, Phenom, Oracle Recruiting, iCIMS, and more). ~59% of the F500.
  • Tier 2 — Website Job Extractor (HTTP+LLM): extracts jobs from custom-built careers pages via Gemini/Groq/OpenRouter. ~27% of the F500.
  • Tier 3 — Website Job Extractor (Browser): Playwright variant auto-triggered for JS-rendered careers pages. ~12% of the F500.
