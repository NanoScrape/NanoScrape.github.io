How to read the index

  • Every country page shows one chart per platform. We never sum across platforms — the same position appears on multiple boards, and adding them would double-count.
  • Counts are what the platform publicly reports for a keyword search, on the snapshot date. Not vacancies. Not hires.
  • Keyword lists are derived from ESCO / ISCO-08 and are frozen per taxonomy version. Any change ships as a new version alongside the old one.
  • Snapshots run every Tuesday 06:00 UTC. Monthly published figures are the mean of that month's weeks.
  • Full per-country CSV and JSON downloads are on the country page.
