Job Market Index — Italian-speaking Switzerland

Coming soon. This page will publish weekly open-position counts for Italian-language job postings in Switzerland (Svizzera italiana), measured directly from each tracked platform. Same methodology as the German-language section: platform-reported totals, no summing, no estimation, weekly snapshots, CC BY 4.0.

What will land here

  • A mega chart of open positions by industry across all tracked Italian-language Swiss platforms.
  • Per-industry charts showing the top job titles and how each platform reports them.
  • CSV + JSON downloads under CC BY 4.0.
  • Same taxonomy version as the German-language section so cross-region comparison is honest.

Why the delay

Each language region needs its own keyword taxonomy (ESCO translations, Italian-side qualifications, culturally-appropriate synonyms) and a region-specific platform set — jobup.ch dominates the Svizzera italiana market and is not yet in the JMI. Once the platform is instrumented and the taxonomy is signed off, the first snapshot ships here.
