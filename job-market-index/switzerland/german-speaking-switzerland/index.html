Job Market Index — German-speaking Switzerland

v3 series — calibration phase. After independent review (2026-09-07) we rebuilt the keyword taxonomy from the ground up: every job title on this page was checked against the actual result feed of jobs.ch and arbeit.swiss, and only titles where at least 70% of returned postings really contain the term are shown. Broad-match keywords (e.g. jobs.ch reporting 411 identical results for Bundesrichter, Kantonsrichter and Amtsrichter) were dropped. The page is noindex until four consecutive weekly snapshots on the new series stabilise; the CSV under every chart carries the v3 methodology today.

Weekly snapshot of German-language job postings across 3 platforms in Switzerland (jobs.ch, arbeit.swiss, jobscout24.ch). Latest snapshot: 2026-09-07. Counts are what each platform publicly reports for the tracked search terms. We measure ads written in German — a German-language posting in a company outside the Deutschschweiz is included; we do not filter by canton. French, Italian-language sections coming next.

Methodology

  • What is measured: job postings visible on a given platform on a given date. Not vacancies, not hires.
  • Counts are what the platform reports, not our own count. Where they differ, we publish the measured deviation.
  • Platform figures are not comparable and are not summed. The same position appears on multiple boards; combined totals would double-count.
  • arbeit.swiss is a public employment service subject to the Swiss Stellenmeldepflicht, which mandates reporting of vacancies in occupations with above-average unemployment. Its occupational composition is systematically different from commercial boards and is not a market-share proxy.
  • Keyword set is derived from ESCO / ISCO-08 and frozen per taxonomy version. Any change ships as a new version and both run in parallel for at least three months so the series can be spliced.
  • Sampling: weekly, Tuesday 06:00 UTC. Job posting volume has a strong weekday cycle; a fixed weekday removes that.
  • Revision policy: published figures are never silently edited. Corrections appear as a dated revision note.
