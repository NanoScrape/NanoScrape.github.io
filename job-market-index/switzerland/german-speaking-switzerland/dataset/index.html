Method changelog

v3 (2026-09-07 — current)
Precision-first Swiss-title taxonomy. Every keyword was sampled against the platform SERP and only titles where at least 70% of returned postings actually contain the term are shown. Broad-match keywords excluded (jobs.ch was reporting 411 identical results for three unrelated judge titles; Software Developer matched 2/100 real titles). Series reset.
v2 (2026-09-07 — deprecated same day)
Dedupe of gender variants (Koch/Köchin no longer double-counted), Swiss-standard terms (Fleischer → Metzger), removal of bucket-floor artefacts. Failed reviewer's precision gate (jobs.ch median 0.11, arbeit.swiss 0.25). Superseded by v3.
v1 (2026-09-03 — archived)
Initial ESCO-based candidate set of ~115 titles per industry. Not intended as a shipping taxonomy.
