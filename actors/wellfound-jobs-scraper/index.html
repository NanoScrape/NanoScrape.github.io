Who should use this

Startup recruiters and talent teams use this actor to monitor the Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) inventory by role keyword and remote flag, keeping their pipeline current on what early-stage and growth-stage companies are hiring for without daily manual browsing.

Job aggregator builders targeting the US startup market use the tags field (SaaS, Fintech, Healthcare, AI/ML) to power faceted filtering on their boards, and the company_url to link through to full company profiles for additional context.

Salary analysts and compensation benchmarkers covering the startup ecosystem extract disclosed pay ranges by role keyword to build offer guidance that accounts for equity-stage dynamics specific to seed and Series A/B companies.
