Pracuj.pl Scraper: Poland's Largest Job Board, 35 Fields

Scrape job listings from Pracuj.pl, Poland's largest job board. Returns 35 fields per job: title, company, company_url, company_website, location, city, region (voivodeship), salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency (PLN), salary_period, employment_type, work_mode (Stacjonarna / Hybrydowa / Zdalna), workload_min and workload_max, description_full, requirements, benefits, technologies, posted_at and apply URL. Search by keyword, location and remote-only filter across all Polish regions.

What you can scrape

  • Listing core: title, company, city, region (16 Polish voivodeships), posted_at
  • Salary (PLN): salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency (PLN), salary_period (monthly/hourly), salary_text
  • Work mode: Stacjonarna (on-site) / Hybrydowa / Zdalna (remote), workload_min and workload_max
  • Full content: description_full, requirements, benefits, technologies array
  • Company intel: company_logo_url, company_url, company_website

Primary use cases

  • Poland tech recruiting. Source Polish developers across Warsaw, Kraków and Wrocław, filtered by technologies array
  • Nearshoring leads. Track Polish companies expanding their tech teams as a signal for nearshoring partnerships
  • PLN salary benchmark. Aggregate salary_min and salary_max by voivodeship to map compensation by Polish region
  • Remote-friendly Polish employers. Filter work_mode = Zdalna to source Polish companies open to fully remote hires
