Who should use this

Defense and government contractors use this actor to identify open federal roles before candidates apply, giving their placement teams advance intelligence on which agencies are actively hiring.

Veteran hiring researchers filter by hiring_path: veterans to measure federal veteran-preference opportunity volume by agency, location, and GS grade.

Federal job board operators and salary benchmarking teams use the occupational series codes and GS pay tables to build structured compensation databases without manual data entry.
