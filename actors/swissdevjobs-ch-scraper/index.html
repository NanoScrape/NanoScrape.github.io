Who should use this

Tech recruiters placing developers in Switzerland use this actor because SwissDevJobs.ch is the only Swiss board organized by technology stack. Filter by Python, Go, ML-AI, or Rust and get directly relevant candidates in one query.

Salary benchmarking teams value the structured CHF salary ranges per seniority level and company type, which are rare on Swiss job boards.

Visa-sponsoring employers can query the visaSponsorship: true filter to find competing roles and benchmark their offer against the market.
