Jobindex.dk Scraper: Denmark's Largest Job Board, 20 Fields

Scrape job listings from Jobindex.dk, Denmark's largest job board. Returns 20 structured fields per job including title, company, location, tags, full description in HTML and text, salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency (DKK), salary_period, posted_at, apply_deadline and apply URL. Covers all industries and regions across Denmark, including Copenhagen, Aarhus, Odense and Aalborg.

What you can scrape

  • Listing core: title, company, location, tags array, posted_at, apply_deadline, scraped_at
  • Salary (DKK): salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency (DKK), salary_period, salary_text
  • Full content: description_full, description_html
  • Apply path: apply_url, source_url, company_logo_url, search_query
  • Regions: Copenhagen, Aarhus, Odense, Aalborg + every Danish region

Primary use cases

  • Denmark hiring intelligence. Track every job posted in Copenhagen this week as an early growth signal for Danish startups
  • Nordic salary research. Combine with the Arbetsformedlingen Sweden scraper to compare DKK and SEK compensation for the same role
  • Apply-by-deadline alerts. Use apply_deadline to schedule candidate outreach before the position closes
  • Tag-based niche sourcing. Filter by the tags array (e.g. fintech, biotech) to build vertical-specific Danish talent pools
