What you can scrape

  • Product identity: name, source_platform, product_image_url
  • Pricing: best_price, price_currency, price_count (number of merchant offers)
  • Social proof: rating, review_count
  • Detail mode: brand, ean, structured specs (storage, color, processor, etc.)
  • Input modes: searchQuery (keyword) or categoryUrl (direct paste)
  • Image preview: product images render in the dataset table view

Primary use cases

  • Price monitoring. Track competitor prices across German e-commerce daily
  • Repricing. Feed best_price into marketplace repricing tools
  • Market research. Analyze pricing trends by category and brand at scale
  • Dropshipping margin scan. Filter by price_count to find products with healthy merchant competition
  • Catalog enrichment. Pull EAN, brand and specs for product database hygiene
