What you can scrape

  • 50+ country coverage: DE, IT, FR, ES, UK, US, BR, IN, AU and many more local Jobrapido domains
  • Salary intel: salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency, salary_period, salary_text
  • Aggregator advantage: source URL resolves to the original employer career page or ATS
  • Listing core: title, company, city, region, country, employment_type, work_mode
  • Workload: workload_min, workload_max, posted_at, scraped_at

Primary use cases

  • Multi-country market scans. Pull 50+ Jobrapido country domains in one run for a global hiring heatmap
  • ATS deep-link discovery. Use the resolved source URL to identify which ATS (Lever, Greenhouse, Workday) the employer uses for follow-up enrichment
  • Italy market entry. Jobrapido was born in Italy and has the strongest IT coverage of any aggregator, ideal for Italy-specific recruiting
  • Backfill missing data. Use Jobrapido as a fallback when more targeted scrapers (Indeed, LinkedIn) miss a country or region
