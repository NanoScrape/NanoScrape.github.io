Who Should Use This

This actor is the specialist tool for design talent agencies, portfolio-driven recruiting platforms, and salary benchmarking services covering the creative industry. Dribbble Jobs is purpose-built for the design community -- every listing is a design role (UI/UX, product, brand, motion, illustration) with no generalist noise. The job_category and experience_level fields normalise role classification across the entire board without any LLM guessing. Companies hiring from Dribbble value portfolio quality over credentials, making this the right source for creative agencies that want to identify and engage design-led hiring teams directly.
