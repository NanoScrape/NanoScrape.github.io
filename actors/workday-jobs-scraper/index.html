Cost math

Priced at $0.001 per job ($1.00 per 1,000), in company mode and in keyword search across companies. Duplicates and filtered-out jobs are not charged. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Keyword search across companies

Leave companies empty and set keywords, and the actor searches a built-in list of 3,662 Workday job sites, biggest hirers first. It answers questions like “which large employers are hiring a data engineer in Germany right now” when you don't know the companies yet. Your keyword goes to each company's own Workday search and is matched against the job title. Narrow the scan with countries, location, postedWithinDays and maxCompanies (default 500). With companies set, the actor reads only the job sites you list (company mode). Both modes return the same fields.
