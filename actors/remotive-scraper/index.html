What You Get

  • salary_min / salary_max / salary_currency / salary_text — structured salary parsed from Remotive's public salary display, rare among curated remote boards
  • category — high-level role category: Software Development, Design, Marketing, DevOps, QA, Customer Support, Finance, and more
  • tags — role keywords and technology tags (e.g. React, Python, Figma) as an array for skill-based filtering
  • job_type — full-time, part-time, contract, or freelance
  • candidate_required_location — location requirement string (e.g. 'Worldwide', 'USA Only', 'Europe Only')
  • company logo_url / company_website / company_tagline — employer branding fields from the optional company enrichment mode
  • company_open_positions_count / company_profile_url — total open roles at the employer; useful for company-level hiring signals
  • apply_url — direct application link; always a real URL, not a redirect through a tracking wall

Who Should Use This

Remote-first job platforms and newsletters use Remotive.com as a high-signal, low-noise source because the board is manually curated, so every listing is a genuine remote role rather than a 'remote-friendly' upsell. Salary benchmark tools targeting the distributed work market value the structured salary fields, which Remotive displays more consistently than most other remote boards. Developer tool and SaaS companies looking to sponsor niche job categories use the category and tags fields to find the exact role clusters their products serve (e.g. DevOps, QA, Data Science). Hiring intelligence platforms use company_open_positions_count to identify companies in active growth mode and prioritize outreach accordingly.
