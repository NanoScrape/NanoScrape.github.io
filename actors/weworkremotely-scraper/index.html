What you can scrape

  • Job details: title, id, full description (text + HTML), category, employment_type, posting date, apply URL
  • Salary intelligence: salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency, salary_text
  • Geography: countries (from listing badges), region (Anywhere, US Only, Europe)
  • Company info: company name, company logo, company profile URL on WeWorkRemotely
  • Employer enrichment: company_hq_location, company_website, total_jobs_posted
  • Meta: source URL, search query, scraped_at timestamp

Primary use cases

  • Remote talent sourcing. Filter by Full-Time vs Contract and feed structured records to your ATS
  • Salary benchmarking by region. Combine salary_min and salary_max with countries to size pay bands per market
  • Remote-friendly company list building. Use company_hq_location plus total_jobs_posted to spot serial remote employers
  • Niche backfill. Ingest description_html into a vertical-specific job board, filter by Programming or Design category
