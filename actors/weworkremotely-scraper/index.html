Who should use this

Remote job board operators use WeWorkRemotely as one of their primary sources because the RSS category feeds are comprehensive, predictable, and fast. The countries field enables geographic filtering without parsing description text.

Salary benchmarking and compensation analysts focused on remote tech roles use the structured salary_min / salary_max fields, which WWR actively encourages employers to publish, making it one of the better remote-salary data sources.

Lead generation teams targeting growing remote-first companies use includeCompanyInfo: true to surface employer HQ, website, and total open positions, identifying which companies are scaling quickly.
