What you get

  • Image basics: width, height, format, color_space, bit_depth, file_size_bytes, mime_type
  • EXIF camera: camera_make, camera_model, lens_model, aperture, shutter_speed, iso, focal_length
  • EXIF time: date_taken, date_digitized, date_modified, timezone_offset
  • GPS: gps_latitude, gps_longitude, gps_altitude, gps_direction, location_country, location_city
  • Rights and tags: copyright, artist, software, iptc_keywords, iptc_caption

Primary use cases

  • OSINT investigations. Geolocate photos from public Telegram, Twitter, or news sources via GPS EXIF
  • Photo forensics. Detect editing software, original capture device, and tampering markers
  • Copyright audits. Bulk-check copyright, artist, and licensing fields across image catalogs
  • Asset cataloging. Index photo libraries by camera, date, location, and IPTC keywords
  • Real estate verification. Confirm listing photos were taken at the claimed property via GPS
