Who Should Use This

This actor is the specialist tool for UK third-sector recruiters, public-sector job aggregators, and outbound charity-sector sales teams. Guardian Jobs is a hand-curated board with far higher signal-to-noise for charity, media, education, and public-sector roles than any generalist aggregator. The employer phone and postal address fields -- retrieved from Guardian's own employer profile pages -- are genuinely rare in job data and turn this into a lead-generation feed as much as a job listing source. Outreach teams combine the employer contact fields with the deep-employer mode to get every open role at a target organisation in a single query.
