Trustpilot Scraper: Business Reviews and Trust Scores

Extract business profiles and consumer reviews from Trustpilot, the global review platform spanning every major industry. Two output types in one run: business profiles (company name, domain, website, trust score, average rating, total review count, categories, country, logo) and individual reviews (1 to 5 star rating, title, full text, author name, author country, total reviews by author, date posted, date of experience, verified purchase status, company reply text and date, source like Organic or Invited). Search by keyword or paste business URLs. Filter by star rating, sort by recency or relevance. We extract only publicly visible review pages, no login bypass.

What you can scrape

  • Business profile: company_name, domain, website, trust_score, average_rating, total_reviews, categories, country, logo
  • Review: star_rating (1 to 5), title, full review text, source (Organic, Invited, ...)
  • Author: name, country, total reviews by author
  • Dates: date posted and date of experience
  • Status: verified purchase boolean
  • Company response: reply text and reply date when present

Primary use cases

  • Brand reputation monitoring. Track trust score and review-volume drift week over week, alert on rating drops
  • Competitor sentiment analysis. Compare review themes (pros, cons, common complaints) across rival businesses
  • E-commerce trust intel. Vet a vendor's Trustpilot trail before procurement or partnership
  • Customer voice mining. Pull 1-star and 5-star reviews into an NLP pipeline for theme extraction and CX research
  • Reply-rate audits. Measure how often a competitor responds to reviews and how fast

Notes and limits

  • Public data only. Trustpilot has anti-scraping ToS language. We extract only publicly visible business and review pages, no login bypass, no private review access
  • Filters supported. Sort by recency or relevance, filter to a specific star rating to isolate 1-star or 5-star reviews
  • Business-only mode. Set maxReviewsPerBusiness: 0 to get only the business summary, useful for cheap bulk profile sweeps
