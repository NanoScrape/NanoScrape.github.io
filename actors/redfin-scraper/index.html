Redfin Scraper: Homes for Sale by City, ZIP or Area

The NanoScrape Redfin Scraper is an Apify actor that extracts real estate listings from redfin.com. It returns 53 fields per result including city, description, url, street_address, unit_number, priced at $0.6/1k redfin listing, with no API key or monthly subscription required.

Get for-sale listings from Redfin search pages: address, price, beds, baths, size, lot size, year built, price per square foot, HOA, status, days on market, property type, photos and open houses (source platform). No login needed.
