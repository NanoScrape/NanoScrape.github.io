What you get

  • Per-link verdict: status_code, error_category, is_broken, response_time_ms
  • Context: source_page, anchor_text, link_type (internal / external / image / script), rel attribute
  • Redirect chain: final_url, redirect_count, redirect_chain[], has_redirect_loop
  • Error detail: error_message, ssl_error, dns_error, timeout_ms_exceeded
  • Crawl controls: max_depth, follow_external, include_assets, max_links_per_page

Primary use cases

  • SEO maintenance. Run a monthly crawl to catch 404s and bad outbound links before they hurt rankings
  • Migration QA. After a site relaunch, verify every internal and external link still resolves to 200
  • Content audits. Identify dead links in old articles for retirement or replacement
  • Link-hygiene monitoring. Alert when a high-traffic outbound link starts returning 5xx or redirecting offsite
  • Vendor portal QA. Continuously test customer-facing portals for broken downloads and asset URLs
