Arbeidsplassen Scraper: Norway Jobs (NAV)

The NanoScrape Arbeidsplassen Jobs Scraper is an Apify actor that extracts job listings from Arbeidsplassen Jobs. It returns 57 fields per result including title, location, city, address, employer_name, priced at $0.9/1k job, with no API key or monthly subscription required.

Scrape job ads from Arbeidsplassen (arbeidsplassen.nav.no), the public job portal of NAV in Norway: title, employer, places, county, dates, deadline and, on request, the full ad text, conditions and contacts. Pay-per-result.
