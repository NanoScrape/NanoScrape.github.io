Who Should Use This

This actor is the specialist tool for UK third-sector recruiters, nonprofit job boards, and charity HR analytics teams. CharityJob is purpose-built for the UK voluntary sector -- every listing is vetted for sector alignment, making signal-to-noise far higher than generalist boards for roles like Head of Fundraising, Programme Director, or Policy Officer. The structured cause_area and category taxonomy is applied by CharityJob's editorial team and cannot be reproduced by scraping generalist boards. Salary benchmarking vendors covering UK nonprofits use the structured GBP salary fields, which come from schema.org JSON-LD and are far more reliable than regex on free text.
