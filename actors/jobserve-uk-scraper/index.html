What you get

  • ir35_status: "inside", "outside", or "undetermined" - structured IR35 classification preserved from the original listing - this field is stripped by every generalist job board
  • contract_length: contract duration in months ("3 months", "6 months", "12 months") as a structured field
  • salary_period: "day" or "year" - critical for contract versus permanent comparison; day-rate listings are the majority on JobServe
  • skills: array of technical skills tags extracted from the listing (React, TypeScript, AWS, Kubernetes, ITIL, etc.)
  • sector: JobServe industry label - IT, Financial Services, Engineering, Public Sector, Hospitality
  • rate_type: "daily" or "annual" - complements salary_period for rate-card analysis
  • company_phone / company_email / company_active_jobs: recruiter contact details and active job count from the recruiter profile page (requires includeCompanyDetails=true)
  • reference: JobServe internal reference number for deduplication across monitoring runs
