Cost math

Priced at $0.0005 per unique job in aggregated feed. 10 unique job in aggregated feeds ≈ $0.01, 100 ≈ $0.05, 1,000 ≈ $0.50. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Start from a Google Maps place list

  • Hiring signals in Google Maps Scraper: if your list of companies comes from Google Maps, you do not need to collect websites first. Turn on enableHiringSignals in the Google Maps Scraper and it sends each place that has a website to Job Feed, then adds hiring_* fields to every place.
  • What each place gets: hiring_status (hiring, no_open_roles, not_read or no_website), hiring_open_roles_count, up to 5 sample role titles in hiring_roles, and the careers page in hiring_jobs_url. not_read means the site could not be checked, not "not hiring".
  • Good for: recruiting agencies and sales teams who search by trade and city first ("dentists in Zurich") and then want to know which of those businesses are hiring.
  • Cost: the add-on adds no Google Maps charge. You pay only the Job Feed actor's own events (and the website and career-site extractors it calls) in your account. In our sample of 40 Swiss and German companies with the default cap of 5 roles, a place that is hiring cost about $0.035 to $0.05, the worst case was about $0.073 per place, and places that could not be read cost about $0. Places without a website, or whose website is a directory or social profile, are never sent and are not charged.
  • Limits: hiringSignalsMaxPlaces (default 50, up to 500) caps how many places are checked per run. Career sites that only work in a browser are skipped unless hiringBrowserFallback is on. Already have a list of company websites? Run Job Feed directly instead.
