Job Feed: 109+ Job Boards, 26+ ATS & Any Company Career Site

The NanoScrape Job Feed Scraper is an Apify actor that extracts structured web data from Job Feed. It returns 64 fields per result including title, company, location, salary, description_text, priced at $0.5/1k unique job in aggregated feed, with no API key or monthly subscription required.

One deduplicated, newest-first job feed from 109+ job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, StepStone, jobs.ch, Arbeitsagentur, Reed, Glassdoor, USAJOBS), 26+ ATS career portals (Workday, SuccessFactors, Greenhouse, Lever, Personio) and any company website. Stable job_id. New boards and ATS added continuously.
