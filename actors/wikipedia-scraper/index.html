Wikipedia Scraper: Articles, Summaries, and Metadata, $0.50 per 1,000 Articles

Extract Wikipedia articles by title, URL, or Wikidata ID via the official MediaWiki API. Returns the article title, summary (first paragraph), full plaintext content, thumbnail image, lead image, all categories, geographic coordinates when applicable, the Wikidata Q-ID, pageviews over the last 30 days, and references to other languages. Supports 12+ languages including English, German, French, Spanish, Chinese, and Japanese. Ideal for AI training data, knowledge graph enrichment, RAG ingestion, and reference databases.

What you get

  • Identity: title, page_id, wikidata_id (Q-ID), canonical_url, language
  • Content: summary (first paragraph), content (full plaintext), sections[], word_count
  • Visual: thumbnail_url, lead_image_url, image_count
  • Taxonomy: categories[], interwiki_links[] (other-language editions)
  • Geographic + popularity: coordinates (lat/lon if geo article), pageviews_30d, edit_count, last_edited_at

Primary use cases

  • AI training data. Build clean, structured corpora for LLM fine-tuning across topics and languages
  • Knowledge graph enrichment. Resolve company, person, or place entities to Wikidata Q-IDs and pull canonical descriptions
  • RAG ingestion. Feed Wikipedia content into vector databases as authoritative reference grounding
  • Multilingual reference databases. Pull the same article in 12+ languages for parallel-text projects
  • Topic monitoring. Track edit_count and last_edited_at to detect surges of public interest
