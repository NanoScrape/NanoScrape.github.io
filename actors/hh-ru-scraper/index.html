HeadHunter (hh.ru) Scraper: Russia & CIS Jobs

Scrape job listings from HeadHunter (hh.ru, hh.kz, hh.uz, hh.by) via the official public REST API (source platform). Returns title, employer (IT-accreditation, rating, website), location, metro stations, RUB/USD/EUR salary with mode/frequency, employment form, work schedule by days, shift hours, civil-law contracts, night shift, internship, key skills, professional roles, and contacts. Russia's #1 job board (90M+ monthly visits) + Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Belarus.
