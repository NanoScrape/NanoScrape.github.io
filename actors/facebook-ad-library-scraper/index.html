Use cases

  • Competitive intelligence — see which ads a competitor runs, with copy, creative, call to action and start date
  • Creative research — study hooks, headlines, CTAs and carousel structures at scale
  • Political ad transparency — collect spend and reach ranges for political and issue ads, as Meta publishes them
  • Market sizing — measure how much advertising a category has per country and platform
  • Lead generation — find companies that advertise on Meta in your vertical, with their ad landing page URLs

Four ways to choose what to scrape

Use any combination of searchUrls (a filtered Ad Library URL copied from your browser), searchQueries (keywords), pageIds (numeric Facebook Page IDs, all ads of a Page) and advertiserNames (brand names, searched as exact phrases). Filters inside a pasted URL take precedence over the filter fields; the filter fields apply to keywords, Page IDs and advertiser names. Meta has no newest-first sort, so sortBy offers impressions and time active.
