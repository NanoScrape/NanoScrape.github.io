Cost math

Priced at $0.0005 per filing delivered ($0.50 per 1,000), company profile included. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Input

Put tickers (AAPL), CIK numbers (320193) or company names in companies; a name uses the best match in the SEC company list. searchQuery searches the text of filings from 2001 on, for example "going concern" in quotes for an exact phrase; with companies given, the search is limited to them. formTypes keeps only these forms, such as 10-K, 8-K or S-1; a form matches exactly, so list 10-K/A too for amendments. dateFrom and dateTo limit the filing date. maxResults (default 100) caps the filings of the run, shared equally between companies. includeOlderFilings also reads the older filing pages of each company. An empty input runs a small sample.
