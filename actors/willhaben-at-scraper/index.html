Willhaben.at Scraper: Austria's #1 Classifieds Marketplace

Scrape classified listings from willhaben.at, Austria's largest online marketplace with 10M+ monthly visitors. Returns title, description, price in EUR, state, district, postcode, GPS coordinates, seller name and type, all image URLs, posted date and source URL straight from search results. Optionally enrich with the full ad description from the listing page. Paste any willhaben.at search URL and the actor handles pagination automatically.

What you can scrape

  • Categories: Marketplace (Kaufen und Verkaufen), Real Estate (Immobilien), Jobs, Autos (Gebrauchtwagen)
  • Search: any willhaben.at URL with filters (category, area, price, make, model, condition)
  • SERP data: title, price in EUR, state, district, postcode, GPS coordinates, seller info, images
  • Detail enrichment: full ad description text
  • Multi-search: paste several URLs in one run for parallel processing
  • Seller flagging: private vs professional (Händler) distinction preserved

Primary use cases

  • Used car arbitrage. Watch BMW, Audi or VW listings under a price ceiling in Vienna, Graz or Linz and act on fresh inventory within minutes
  • Second-hand price intel. Track average EUR prices for iPhones, MacBooks or ski equipment across the Austrian market over time
  • Real estate monitoring. Pull Mietwohnungen and Eigentumswohnungen listings in any Bezirk with full filters preserved
  • Dealer competitor watch. Snapshot a Händler's storefront listings daily to spot price changes
  • Job market mapping. Track open positions in any Austrian city by category and salary range
