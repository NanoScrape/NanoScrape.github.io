Who Should Use This

This actor is ideal for US local staffing agencies, gig-economy researchers, and B2B lead generators who need the informal job market that never appears on Indeed or LinkedIn. Craigslist is where small businesses, local trades employers, and independent contractors post without HR systems -- the direct email and phone fields extracted from posting bodies are the primary differentiator. Hyper-local hiring analysts use the 430+ city subdomain coverage and geo-coordinate fields to map hiring density by neighbourhood. The gig category (/ggg/) provides visibility into the US freelance and short-term work market that no traditional job board indexes.
