What you get

  • WHOIS: registrar, registrant_org, creation_date, expiry_date, updated_at, registrant_country, status_codes[]
  • DNS A/AAAA: ipv4_addresses[], ipv6_addresses[], cname_target
  • Mail records: mx_records[], primary_mx, email_provider (Google, Microsoft 365, Zoho)
  • Security records: spf_record, has_spf, dmarc_record, dmarc_policy, dkim_selectors[]
  • Other DNS: ns_records[], txt_records[], soa_record, has_dnssec

Primary use cases

  • B2B prospecting. Enrich 50,000 leads with email provider, registrar age, and parent organization
  • Security audits. Identify domains with weak or missing SPF / DMARC across a portfolio
  • M&A research. Inventory the full domain estate of a target company including aliases and ccTLDs
  • Email-deliverability cleanup. Verify sender domains have SPF + DMARC + DKIM before outbound campaigns
  • Brand monitoring. Track lookalike domain registrations and their hosting infrastructure
