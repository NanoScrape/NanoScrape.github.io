What you can scrape

  • Job listings: title, company, location, canton, employment_type, workload (Pensum), posted_at
  • Salary intelligence: salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency (CHF), salary_text where published
  • Full job content: description, requirements, company_benefits, contact_person, contact_email
  • Trust signals: company logo, source_url, apply_url, scraped_at timestamp

Primary use cases

  • Swiss talent market mapping. Pull JobScout24 weekly to map hiring volume by canton, role and seniority across Switzerland
  • Recruiter prospecting. Identify Swiss SMEs hiring right now (a strong growth signal) and feed the list into your CRM
  • Salary benchmarking. Aggregate salary_min and salary_max across cantons to size offers vs. the Swiss market
  • Aggregator backfill. Ingest job descriptions and company benefits into your own job board or ATS

Notes and limits

  • Honest scale. JobScout24 carries roughly thousands of active Swiss listings at any time, not millions. Best used as one input among several Swiss boards
  • Salary fields are often empty. Many Swiss SMEs do not publish salary in the listing itself
  • Contact email present on a subset of postings. Direct employers more likely than recruitment agencies
