What you can scrape

  • Seller profile: sellerName, businessName, business address (street, city, state, postalCode, country)
  • Ratings by period: ratingsAvg, ratingsCount, % positive 30d / 90d / 12mo / lifetime
  • Policies: returnPolicySummary, shippingPolicySummary, about, sellerSince
  • Seller-catalog products: asin, title, price, stars, reviews, position, isPrime, isFulfilledByAmazon
  • Two row types in one dataset: profile rows plus product rows, filterable via the type field

Primary use cases

  • Seller research. Find competitors selling in your category, see their ratings and policies
  • Catalog audit. Pull every product a seller offers for SKU-level competitive analysis
  • Trust signal evaluation. Compare ratings across periods for risk-screening 3rd-party sellers
  • Partnership prospecting. Find sellers with strong ratings in adjacent categories
