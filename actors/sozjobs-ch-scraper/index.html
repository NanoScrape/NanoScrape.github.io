Sozjobs.ch Scraper: Swiss Social Work, Healthcare and Education Jobs

Scrape job listings from Sozjobs.ch, the leading Swiss job portal for social work, healthcare, education, childcare, and care roles in German-speaking Switzerland. Filter by region and contract type. Returns title, company, location, canton, workload (Pensum), contact person, and full description. $3.00 per 1,000 jobs.

What You Get

  • title, company, location — with canton auto-detected for all German-speaking Swiss cantons
  • workload_min / workload_max — Pensum percentage as structured integers; critical for Swiss healthcare part-time roles (common at 40-80%)
  • sector / category — Sozjobs.ch sector classification: social work, Pflege, childcare, education, disability care, addiction services
  • salary_text and salary range in CHF — extracted from job descriptions where disclosed by the employer
  • contact_person / contact_email / contact_phone — HR contact details from job descriptions
  • employment_type — full-time, part-time, temporary, internship normalized
  • company_description / company_benefits — employer enrichment from the company page section
  • job_status — online / offline for still-alive URL monitoring

Who Should Use This

Swiss healthcare and social sector recruitment platforms need Sozjobs.ch because it is the dominant specialized board for Sozialwesen, Pflege, Erziehung, and Betreuung roles in German-speaking Switzerland — a sector that rarely advertises on generalist boards like Jobs.ch or Indeed. Nonprofit and NGO talent teams covering the Swiss social sector use this actor to monitor open positions at Stiftungen, Sozialinstitutionen, and cantonal care providers across all German-speaking cantons. Workload-filter use case: Swiss healthcare often runs at 60%, 80%, or 100% Pensum; filtering by workloadMin at the input level avoids downloading thousands of full-time listings when you need only part-time posts. Sector-specific analytics tools use the Sozjobs category classification to segment data by sector (addiction services, mental health, elderly care, etc.) without building custom keyword filters.
