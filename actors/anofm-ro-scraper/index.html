ANOFM.ro Scraper: Romania Government Jobs & Vacancies

Scrape official job listings from Romania's National Employment Agency (ANOFM, mediere.anofm.ro). Returns occupation title, COR code, employer, Romanian county (județ), salary in RON, employment type, education level, and contact details for all 41 județe.

What You Get

  • cor_code / cor_name: Romania's official COR occupation classification (ISCO-08 aligned) -- unique to ANOFM and essential for cross-referencing national labour statistics
  • salary_min / salary_max in RON: Structured gross salary in Romanian leu with salary_type (gross/net) -- mandatory for employers registering vacancies with ANOFM
  • company_tax_code: Romanian CIF (fiscal identification number) for every employer -- enables direct cross-referencing with the national company registry
  • available_for_eu_citizens: EURES eligibility flag -- true for positions open to workers from any EU/EEA country via the European Employment Services network
  • contact_name / contact_emails / contact_phones: Recruiter contact details returned by the ANOFM API (no extra fetch required)
  • open_positions_new / open_positions_freed: Demand velocity split showing whether positions are newly created or re-opened vacancies
  • target_group: Subsidised employment programme flags (PCLM, SLD, NEET) for sourcing government-incentivised hires
  • work_regime: Workplace arrangement in Romanian (Hibrid, Telemunca, Sediu) -- normalised output alongside employment_type
