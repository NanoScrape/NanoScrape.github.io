Who should use this

European tech and startup recruiters use this actor because Welcome to the Jungle is the dominant modern job board across France, Germany, and Spain, with richer company branding and salary transparency than traditional boards. At $1 per 1,000 jobs it is the most cost-effective route into this inventory.

Employer branding analysts use the company_benefits, company_size, and company_sector fields to benchmark culture signals across startup competitors in their market.

Salary benchmarking teams covering the French and Spanish markets value the structured salary_min / salary_max fields, which WTTJ encourages employers to publish, making it one of the best sources of disclosed compensation data in Europe.
