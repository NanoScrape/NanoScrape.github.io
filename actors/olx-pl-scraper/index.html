OLX.pl Scraper: Poland's #1 Classifieds Marketplace

Scrape classified listings from olx.pl, Poland's largest online marketplace. The actor uses the public OLX REST API to return title, full description, price in PLN or EUR, condition, city and region, GPS coordinates, seller name and type (private or business), all image URLs, posted date and source URL. Search by keyword, narrow by Polish city (warszawa, krakow, wroclaw) or by category ID, and run multiple queries in parallel. Pagination is automatic.

What you can scrape

  • All categories: phones, electronics, fashion, real estate (nieruchomosci), cars, jobs, services
  • Search: keyword + optional city slug (warszawa, krakow, wroclaw) + optional category ID
  • REST API: direct hits to OLX's public listings endpoint, no HTML parsing
  • Sorting: filter_float_price:asc, filter_float_price:desc, created_at:desc
  • Multi-search: paste several queries in one run, with per-query result caps
  • Seller distinction: private vs business flag preserved

Primary use cases

  • Used phone arbitrage. Watch iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy S24 or Pixel 8 listings under a PLN ceiling in Warsaw, Krakow or Wroclaw
  • Second-hand price intel. Track average PLN prices for laptops, bicycles or PlayStations across Poland over time
  • Dealer competitor watch. Snapshot a business seller's storefront daily to spot price changes
  • Cross-CEE arbitrage. Compare Polish listings against Bazos.sk or Bazos.cz equivalents on neighbouring marketplaces
  • Job market mapping. Pull open positions in any Polish city by category
