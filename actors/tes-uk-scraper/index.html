Tes.com UK Education Jobs Scraper

Extract teaching jobs from Tes.com, the UK's leading education job board. Returns title, employer, salary, teaching stage (primary/secondary/sixth form), subject, position type, contract, start date, closing date, and school type across UK and international schools.

What You Can Scrape

  • Two contact popups extracted: per-vacancy recruiter email/phone AND school-wide contact info, which competitors miss
  • Teaching stage: primary, secondary, sixth-form, nursery, all-through
  • Subject: Maths, English, Science, SEND, MFL, PE, Art, Music, Drama, Computing, Business, RE, History, Geography
  • Position type: leadership, classroom, support, HLTA, SEN
  • Contract type: permanent, fixed-term, supply, maternity
  • School profile enrichment: About text, phase, funding status, gender intake, age range, denomination, gallery images

What you get

  • title + employer_name + employer_logo_url — role title, school name, and logo
  • teaching_stage + subject + position_type — curriculum stage, subject, and role type
  • salary_range — min, max, currency, period from schema.org JobPosting data
  • job_contact_email + job_contact_phone — per-vacancy recruiter contact (unique to this actor)
  • company_phone + company_website — school-wide contact from School contact details popup
  • contract_type + start_date + closing_at — employment terms and key dates
  • company_address_street / city / postcode — full postal address from schema.org
  • company_active_jobs — other open roles at the same school (with includeCompanyDetails)

Who should use this

Teaching recruitment agencies use this actor to reach the exact recruiter responsible for each vacancy via job_contact_email, rather than routing every application through the school's central mailbox and losing it in the pile.

MATs and school groups benchmark competitor pay bands, contract types, and subject demand across their region using the structured salary and subject fields.

Ed-tech vendors building account lists of UK schools by size, type, and phase use company_phase, company_funding_status, and company_age_range to segment their target market without additional data enrichment.
