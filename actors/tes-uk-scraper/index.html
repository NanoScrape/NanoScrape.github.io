Tes.com UK Education Jobs Scraper

Extract teaching jobs from Tes.com, the UK's leading education job board (source platform). Returns title, employer, salary, teaching stage (primary/secondary/sixth form), subject, position type, contract, start date, closing date, school type, plus BOTH school-wide contact info (website, phone, address, about) AND per-vacancy contact (email, phone) that Tes exposes in its two on-page popups.

Cost math

Priced at $0.0060 per detail result. 10 detail results ≈ $0.06, 100 ≈ $0.60, 1,000 ≈ $6.00. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Who should use this

Teaching recruitment agencies use this actor to reach the exact recruiter responsible for each vacancy via job_contact_email, rather than routing every application through the school's central mailbox and losing it in the pile.

MATs and school groups benchmark competitor pay bands, contract types, and subject demand across their region using the structured salary and subject fields.

Ed-tech vendors building account lists of UK schools by size, type, and phase use company_phase, company_funding_status, and company_age_range to segment their target market without additional data enrichment.
