Who Should Use This

This actor is built for US tech recruiters, salary benchmarking tools, and employer-brand analytics platforms. Built In is the only major US tech board that consistently captures structured salary ranges, tech stacks, and company culture context alongside job listings -- the company_tech_stack field alone is not available from any equivalent scraper. HR analytics vendors use it to correlate tech stack and salary ranges across cities and experience levels. Job aggregators targeting the US tech market value the work_arrangement field for remote-first filtering, which Built In applies consistently across all categories.
