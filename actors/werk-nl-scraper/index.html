Werk.nl Scraper: Netherlands UWV Government Jobs & Vacatures

Extract job listings from werk.nl, the Netherlands' national government employment service (UWV Werkbedrijf). Returns title, company, location, province, employment type, salary, full description and contact details. Covers all 12 Dutch provinces.

What You Can Scrape

  • Official UWV source: government-verified listings, no third-party aggregation
  • 12 Dutch provinces: Noord-Holland, Zuid-Holland, Utrecht, Noord-Brabant, Gelderland, and 7 more
  • Dutch contract terminology mapped to English: Vast, Tijdelijk, Stage, Vrijwilligerswerk, Detachering, Leerbaan
  • JSON API access: two-step anonymous session bootstrap, no browser required
  • Study level field: MBO, HBO/bachelor, WO/master required education level
  • Contact extraction: emails, phones, and external URLs parsed from description body

What you get

  • title + company_name + company_website — job title, employer name, and website when available
  • region — Dutch province (Noord-Holland, Zuid-Holland, Utrecht, etc.)
  • employment_type — permanent, temporary, internship, volunteer, secondment, apprenticeship
  • study_level — required education (MBO, HBO/bachelor, WO/master)
  • salary_min / salary_max / salary_period / salary_text — structured EUR pay range
  • min_hours / max_hours — weekly hours range
  • contact_emails + contact_phones — extracted from description
  • apply_url — direct application link

Who should use this

Dutch recruitment agencies and staffing firms use this actor to access the official UWV government job inventory, which includes listings that do not appear on private boards, at $3 per 1,000 jobs with no subscription.

Netherlands job board operators covering the local market rely on the province-level region field and Dutch contract-type mapping to power faceted search across all 12 provinces.

HR analytics teams tracking Dutch salary benchmarks by education level and region use the structured study_level and salary_min / salary_max fields to build compensation reports without manual data collection.
