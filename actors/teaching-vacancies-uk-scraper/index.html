Who should use this

Teaching recruitment agencies aggregate live vacancies from the official DfE source into their candidate CRM, with school contact emails extracted directly from description text for outreach.

EdTech vendors building live demand maps for specific subjects (Maths, Science, SEND) use the subject and phase fields to segment by curriculum area and local authority.

MAT central offices monitor competitor vacancy volume and leadership pay bands using pay_scale and trust_name fields without manual site browsing.
