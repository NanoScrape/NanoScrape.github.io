MyJob.ch Scraper - Swiss Jobs with CHF Salary and Workload Filters

Scrape job listings from MyJob.ch, a Swiss regional job portal focused on Central Switzerland. Filter by canton, workload percentage (Pensum), and company type. Exclude recruitment agencies to get direct employer postings. Returns title, salary (CHF), contact person, workload, and full description. $3.00 per 1,000 jobs.

What You Get

  • title, company, location — with canton resolved automatically from any city or district name
  • salary_text / salary range in CHF — extracted from job descriptions where published
  • workload_min / workload_max — Pensum percentage (e.g. 80-100%) as structured integers; filterable at input time
  • contact_person / contact_email / contact_phone — HR contact details extracted from job bodies
  • company_description / company_benefits / company_social_urls — employer enrichment from the company profile
  • HR company filter — set companyFilter: exclude-hr to return only direct employer postings, removing staffing noise
  • job_status — online / offline for still-alive checks in direct URL mode
  • search_query — tracks which keyword found each result across multi-query runs

Who Should Use This

Swiss recruitment platforms and HR software companies covering the Central Switzerland market need MyJob.ch because it serves St. Gallen, Thurgau, Appenzell, and Graubunden postings that national portals like Jobs.ch often underrepresent. The Pensum (workload) filter is a uniquely Swiss requirement: many employers post at 60-80% or 80-100% part-time, and being able to filter by workloadMin at the input level avoids post-processing. Staffing agencies use companyFilter: exclude-hr to surface only direct employer postings, eliminating competing agency listings from the results. HR contact prospectors use the contact_person and contact_email fields to build outreach lists directly from job postings without additional scraping steps.
