What you get

  • Per-item: title, link, author, published_at, updated_at, content_html, summary
  • Taxonomy: categories[], tags[], language
  • Media: enclosures[] (podcasts, attachments), inline_images[], featured_image
  • Feed metadata: feed_title, feed_description, feed_link, feed_updated_at, total_items
  • Filtering: max_age_days, since_date, category_filter, keyword_filter

Primary use cases

  • News monitoring. Aggregate hundreds of industry sources into a single feed-mention pipeline
  • Podcast indexing. Pull every episode from a podcast catalog with metadata and audio URLs
  • Blog tracking. Diff a competitor's feed daily to detect new posts and topics
  • Brand-mention alerts. Filter feeds by keyword and trigger Slack or webhook notifications
  • Content database hydration. Backfill an editorial database from publisher RSS archives
