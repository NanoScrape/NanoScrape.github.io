Praca.gov.pl Scraper: Poland's Official National Job Database (CBOP)

Scrape verified job listings from praca.gov.pl, Poland's Central Job Offers Database (CBOP) operated by the Ministry of Family and Social Policy. Get company NIP (VAT ID), GPS coordinates, local PUP office details, disability accessibility flags, PFRON-funded positions, salary (PLN), and 40+ government-grade fields. Covers all 16 Polish voivodeships plus EU/EURES listings. $3.00 per 1,000 jobs.

What You Get

  • company_nip — Polish VAT/tax ID; unique to government job databases, enables direct company lookup and deduplication
  • latitude / longitude — GPS coordinates of the workplace for geo-analytics and map-based job search features
  • pup / pup_email — local Powiatowy Urzad Pracy office name and contact email; required for CBOP reference resolution
  • job_number — CBOP reference number (e.g. StPr/26/0659) for legal compliance and audit trails
  • disability_accessible / positions_for_disabled / pfron_funded — three disability-inclusive employment flags unique to CBOP government data
  • voivodeship / powiat / address_full / postal_code / street_address — full administrative geography down to powiat (county) level
  • salary_min / salary_max / salary_currency / salary_period — structured PLN salary; always PLN, always monthly
  • contact_person / contact_emails / contact_phones / application_method — all application channels including direct employer contact

Who Should Use This

Polish HR tech platforms and compliance-focused aggregators need CBOP data because it is the only job source in Poland that carries verified company NIP numbers, enabling cross-reference with the KRS company register and VAT database. Disability employment services and PFRON partners use the pfron_funded, disability_accessible, and positions_for_disabled fields, which exist nowhere else in the Polish job market. Labour-market researchers and government analytics teams use voivodeship and powiat geography plus GPS coordinates to build regional employment heatmaps at the county level. EU mobility programs and EURES partners filter for job_scope: UE to surface the cross-border listings contributed by partner employment offices across Europe.
