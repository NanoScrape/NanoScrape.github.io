Who Should Use This

German HR tech platforms and AI-powered job-matching tools value Stellenmarkt.de because it is one of the few German boards that publishes Aufgaben, Anforderungen, and Wir-bieten as separate structured sections rather than a single HTML blob. Getting requirements and responsibilities as clean arrays removes a scraping-and-parsing step that typically adds a day of engineering work per board. Compensation research platforms covering the German Mittelstand market use the EUR salary fields from schema.org metadata, which Stellenmarkt populates more consistently than smaller regional boards. Job aggregators building DACH coverage pair this actor with the Stellenanzeigen.de Scraper, Arbeitsagentur.de Scraper, and StepStone Scraper to achieve near-complete German market coverage across employer types and posting channels. Skill gap analytics tools use the requirements array as a direct model feature without additional text parsing.
