Stellenmarkt.de Scraper: German Jobs with Structured Requirements, Responsibilities and Benefits

Scrape job listings from Stellenmarkt.de, a leading German national job board serving Europe's largest labor market (45M workers, 3.5M open roles). Get title, company, salary (EUR), structured requirements, responsibilities and benefits as clean arrays (Aufgaben / Anforderungen / Wir bieten), work mode, contact email and phone, and Bundesland code. $3.00 per 1,000 jobs.

What You Get

  • requirements — structured array from the 'Anforderungen' section; ready for skill extraction and NLP without HTML parsing
  • responsibilities — structured array from the 'Aufgaben/Aufgabenbereich' section; what the hire will actually do day-to-day
  • company_benefits — structured array from 'Wir bieten'; perks, pension, remote options extracted as clean strings
  • salary_min / salary_max / salary_currency / salary_period — parsed from schema.org JobPosting metadata embedded in detail pages
  • region / city — German Bundesland extracted from schema.org data; auto-tagged without regex or lookup tables
  • employment_type — Vollzeit, Teilzeit, Praktikum, Ausbildung, Befristet, Freelance normalized from Stellenmarkt's own categories
  • contact_email / contact_phone — extracted from the job body for direct recruiter outreach
  • company_logo_url / company_url — employer logo and profile link for every posting

Who Should Use This

German HR tech platforms and AI-powered job-matching tools value Stellenmarkt.de because it is one of the few German boards that publishes Aufgaben, Anforderungen, and Wir-bieten as separate structured sections rather than a single HTML blob. Getting requirements and responsibilities as clean arrays removes a scraping-and-parsing step that typically adds a day of engineering work per board. Compensation research platforms covering the German Mittelstand market use the EUR salary fields from schema.org metadata, which Stellenmarkt populates more consistently than smaller regional boards. Job aggregators building DACH coverage pair this actor with the Stellenanzeigen.de Scraper, Arbeitsagentur.de Scraper, and StepStone Scraper to achieve near-complete German market coverage across employer types and posting channels. Skill gap analytics tools use the requirements array as a direct model feature without additional text parsing.
