Yahoo Finance Markets Scraper: Stocks, ETFs and Crypto Lists

The NanoScrape Yahoo Finance Markets Scraper is an Apify actor that exports the public market lists of finance.yahoo.com. It returns 34 fields per row including symbol, name, price, change, volume, market cap, P/E ratio and 52-week range as numbers with the raw text kept, priced at $0.90 per 1,000 rows, with no API key or monthly subscription required. The data is for research and is not financial advice.

Read the public market lists of Yahoo Finance (most active, gainers, losers, trending, 52-week gainers and losers, most active ETFs and all crypto currencies) and get one record per row with the numbers already parsed: price, change, volume, market cap, P/E and 52-week range, with the raw text kept alongside (source platform). Input

Put list names in lists: most-active, gainers, losers, trending, 52-week-gainers, 52-week-losers, etf-most-active and crypto-all; each list is read in turn. maxResults (default 100, maximum 1,000) is the most rows from each list, up to the list total. Other list names are refused before the run starts. Large values come as numbers (4.314T becomes 4314000000000) with the raw text in fields such as marketCapRaw; values the page shows as -- come back as null. An empty input reads most-active.

Cost math

Priced at $0.0009 per row saved to the dataset ($0.90 per 1,000): 100 rows ≈ $0.09, 1,000 rows ≈ $0.90. The same row price applies to stock, ETF and crypto rows. A symbol that appears twice in the same list is returned and charged once, and an interrupted run continues without charging rows twice. The run ends cleanly at your maximum total charge. No monthly subscription, you pay only for what you extract.

FAQ

How much does the yahoo-finance-markets-scraper cost?

Priced at $0.0009 per row saved to the dataset ($0.90 per 1,000). The same row price applies to stock, ETF and crypto rows. No monthly subscription, you only pay for what you extract.

What fields does it return?

34 fields: symbol, name, asset class, list and rank, quote URL, currency, price, change and change percent, volume, 3-month average volume, market cap, P/E ratio, 52-week change and range, with the raw text kept next to the numbers. ETF rows add moving averages and returns, crypto rows add 24-hour volume and circulating supply.

Is this financial advice?

No. The actor exports the public market lists as data. The data is for research and is not investment advice. Prices are as the site shows them at the moment the page was read and can be delayed.

Why both a number and a raw text?

The page writes large values in short form. You get the number for calculations and the raw text, such as 4.314T, to compare with the page.

How many rows can I get per list?

Up to the list total or maxResults, whichever is smaller, with a ceiling of 1,000 per list. The trending list has one page of 100 rows, and the gainers list is short on some days. The crypto list has thousands of rows and is read in list order.

Does it return single quotes, news or history?

No. Only the market list pages are read. Single quote pages, news, charts, price history and financial statements are not collected.

Do I need an API key from the source platform?

No. No Yahoo account and no API key. Give the actor list names and it does the rest.
