Kleinanzeigen.de Scraper: Germany's #1 Classifieds (50M+ visitors)

Scrape classified listings from kleinanzeigen.de (formerly eBay Kleinanzeigen), Germany's largest online marketplace with 50M+ monthly visitors. Returns title, EUR price with type flag (festpreis, VB, zu_verschenken), location with postcode, top-ad flag, all image URLs, posted date and source URL. Optionally enrich with the full description, category path, seller info (private vs commercial) and shipping availability from the detail page. Paste any kleinanzeigen.de search URL and the actor handles pagination.

What you can scrape

  • Categories: electronics, furniture, cars, real estate, fashion, services, baby and kids, and more
  • Search: any kleinanzeigen.de URL with filters (category, city, postcode radius, price, condition)
  • Smart price parsing: handles VB (negotiable), Zu verschenken (free), and German thousands separator
  • Detail enrichment: full description, category path, seller name and type, shipping availability
  • Multi-search: paste several URLs in one run for parallel processing
  • Top-ad flag: distinguish promoted ads from organic results

Primary use cases

  • Used car arbitrage. Watch VW, BMW, Mercedes listings under a price ceiling and act on fresh inventory within minutes
  • Second-hand price intel. Track average EUR prices for iPhones, MacBooks, bikes or PlayStations across major German cities over time
  • Free items sweep. Monitor "Zu verschenken" listings for valuable freebies in Berlin, Hamburg, or Munich
  • Dealer competitor watch. Snapshot a commercial seller's storefront daily to spot price changes
  • Furniture and appliance flips. Find under-priced listings within a postcode radius for resale
