What You Can Scrape

  • Business profiles — name, category, address (with parsed street/city/postal code/state/country), phone, website, Google Maps URL
  • Ratings breakdown — average rating, review count, and reviews-per-rating distribution (1 to 5 stars)
  • Featured reviews (opt-in) — author name, star rating, publication date, review text, owner responses
  • Opening hours — full weekly schedule with multiple slots per day, current open/closed status
  • Popular times (opt-in) — visitor-traffic histogram by weekday and hour (0 to 100)
  • GPS + geo data — latitude, longitude, Plus Code, IANA timezone
  • Photos and menu — thumbnail, image gallery, menu link, reservation and order-online links
  • Attributes — dine-in, delivery, Wi-Fi, wheelchair access, and other Google-listed amenities

Optional Enrichments

  • includeReviews — fetch featured reviews per place with author, star rating, date, text, and owner response. Configure with maxReviewsPerPlace, reviewsSort, and reviewsNewerThan. Adds ~300 ms per place. Free.
  • includePopularTimes — pull Google's visitor-traffic histogram by weekday and hour. Adds ~1.5 s per place. Free.
  • enableContactExtraction — AI-powered add-on that visits each business website and extracts team member names, emails, phones, and departments. Billed by the Website Contact Extractor.
  • enableJobExtraction — AI-powered add-on that scans each business's career page and returns open positions. Billed by the Website Job Extractor.
  • excludeCids — pass an array of Google CIDs to skip places you have already collected. Perfect for incremental scheduled runs.
  • Structured queries — instead of plain searchStrings, use the queries array to attach a per-query company_id and separate the search term from the location and country code.
