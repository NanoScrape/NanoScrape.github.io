Cost math

Priced at $0.001 per job when you list the companies ($1.00 per 1,000), or $0.0015 per job from keyword search across companies ($1.50 per 1,000). Duplicates and filtered-out jobs are not charged. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Keyword search across companies

Leave companies empty and set keywords, and the actor searches a built-in list of 10,550 companies on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, biggest hirers first. It answers questions like “which companies are hiring a data engineer in Germany right now” when you don't know the companies yet. Keywords are matched against the job title. Narrow the scan with countries, location, postedWithinDays and maxCompanies (default 500). With companies set (board URLs or company names), the actor reads only those boards (company mode). Both modes return the same fields; keyword search costs $0.0015 per job, company mode $0.001.
