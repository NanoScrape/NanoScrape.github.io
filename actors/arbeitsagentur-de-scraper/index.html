Who Should Use This

This actor is designed for German HR tech vendors, recruitment agencies, and labour market research firms that need the official government vacancy database. The Bundesagentur fur Arbeit holds over 1 million active listings -- the most comprehensive official source for Germany. Sales teams and B2B data providers value the recruiter email and phone fields from the listing detail pages. Compared to commercial boards like StepStone or Indeed-DE, this actor surfaces employer logos, employer profile descriptions, and official Homepage links that companies register directly with the BA.
