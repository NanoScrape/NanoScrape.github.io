What You Get

  • contact_email / contact_phone — extracted from data-contact-match attributes in detail pages; real, unobscured employer contact details when provided
  • employer_questions — screening questions the employer asks applicants (e.g. 'Do you have current AHPRA registration?'); unique to Seek/JobStreet/JobsDB
  • company_perks — employer-listed benefits as an array: salary packaging, fitness passport, career development, and more
  • content_hash — MD5 of the listing content for change detection; enables incremental update pipelines without re-fetching unchanged listings
  • salary_min / salary_max / salary_currency / salary_period — structured across all 8 country currencies (AUD, NZD, SGD, MYR, IDR, PHP, HKD, THB)
  • work_arrangement — On-site / Hybrid / Remote; consistently populated across all 8 platform variants
  • classification / sub_classification — Seek's formal category taxonomy (e.g. Healthcare and Medical / Nursing - Emergency)
  • job_status — online / expired for still-alive monitoring in direct URL mode

Who Should Use This

Asia-Pacific job aggregators and HR tech platforms covering multiple APAC countries need this actor because it is the only scraper that covers all three platform variants (Seek, JobStreet, JobsDB) under a single unified schema, eliminating the need to maintain three separate scrapers with three different output formats. Healthcare and nursing recruitment platforms get employer_questions and contact_email, which are unique to the Seek family and enable pre-screening and direct outreach at the point of scraping. Salary benchmark tools covering the APAC region value the multi-currency salary parsing: AUD, NZD, SGD, MYR, IDR, PHP, HKD, and THB are all extracted and normalized in the same schema. Job monitoring pipelines use the content_hash field to detect listing changes without re-fetching the full detail page on every run.
