Cost math

Priced at $0.0025 per job. 10 jobs ≈ $0.03, 100 ≈ $0.25, 1,000 ≈ $2.50. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Who Should Use This

Asia-Pacific job aggregators and HR tech platforms covering multiple APAC countries need this actor because it is the only scraper that covers all three platform variants (Seek, JobStreet, JobsDB) under a single unified schema, eliminating the need to maintain three separate scrapers with three different output formats. Healthcare and nursing recruitment platforms get employer_questions and contact_email, which are unique to the Seek family and enable pre-screening and direct outreach at the point of scraping. Salary benchmark tools covering the APAC region value the multi-currency salary parsing: AUD, NZD, SGD, MYR, IDR, PHP, HKD, and THB are all extracted and normalized in the same schema. Job monitoring pipelines use the content_hash field to detect listing changes without re-fetching the full detail page on every run.
