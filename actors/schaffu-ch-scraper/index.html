Schaffu.ch Scraper - Schaffhausen Region Jobs with CHF Salary and Workload Filter

Scrape job listings from Schaffu.ch, the regional job portal for Schaffhausen and surrounding Swiss cantons. Filter by workload percentage (Pensum) and company type. Exclude recruitment agencies to get direct employer postings. Returns title, salary (CHF), contact person, and full description. $3.00 per 1,000 jobs.

What You Get

  • title, company, location — with canton auto-resolved via the Schaffu API from any district or city name
  • workload_min / workload_max — Pensum percentage as structured integers; filter at input time with workloadMin / workloadMax
  • salary_text and salary range in CHF — extracted from job descriptions where published by the employer
  • contact_person / contact_email / contact_phone — HR contact extracted from job bodies
  • company_description / company_benefits — employer profile enrichment from the company page
  • HR company filter — exclude-hr removes staffing agency listings; only-hr shows only agencies
  • job_status — online / offline for still-alive URL checks in direct URL mode
  • search_query tracking — each result carries the keyword that found it for multi-query attribution

Who Should Use This

Swiss HR platforms and regional recruiters covering the Schaffhausen canton use Schaffu.ch as their primary local source because it captures postings from Schaffhausen employers that national Swiss boards like Jobs.ch or Indeed do not index at the same depth. The Pensum workload filter lets you target the common 60-80% and 80-100% Swiss part-time job categories at input time, avoiding the need to filter thousands of results post-scrape. Direct employer outreach teams use companyFilter: exclude-hr to remove competing staffing agency listings and extract contact_person and contact_email for direct hiring manager outreach. Still-alive monitoring pipelines use direct URL mode to verify whether previously scraped listings remain active without re-crawling the full site.
