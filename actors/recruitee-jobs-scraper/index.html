Cost math

Priced at $0.001 per job ($1.00 per 1,000). Filtered-out and duplicate jobs are not charged; a career site that is not found costs nothing. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Input

Put career sites in companies: https://{company}.recruitee.com, a posting URL (/o/{job-title}, reduced to its career site) or the bare company name (bunq is https://bunq.recruitee.com). keywords keeps jobs whose title matches a keyword, and location or countries (ISO-2 codes such as CH) keep jobs in a place; keywords filter the sites you list, they do not search other companies. postedWithinDays drops older jobs, sortBy is newest (default) or relevance. maxJobsPerCompany (default 50) and maxResults (default 100) cap the run, 0 for no limit. includeDescription (default on) adds the description HTML. URLs of other job systems are skipped and never charged. An empty input reads one sample career site.
