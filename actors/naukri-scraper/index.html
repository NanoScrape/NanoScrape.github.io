Cost math

Who Should Use This

Recruitment agencies and HR tech platforms covering the Indian and Gulf markets need AmbitionBox ratings and company tags to help candidates evaluate employers — data that Naukri's own export feature does not include. Sales teams targeting Indian enterprises use company_tags (Forbes Global 2000, Foreign MNC) and company_similar_companies to build segmented prospect lists directly from job data. Compensation benchmarking tools value the ISO 4217 currency normalization across INR, USD, and AED, which makes cross-platform salary analysis possible without manual currency mapping. Job aggregators serving the India and MENA region get a deduplicated, 45-field feed from a single actor call across both Naukri and NaukriGulf.
