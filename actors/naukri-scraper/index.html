What You Get

  • title, company, company logo, location — with city-level location cleaning and optional localities array
  • salary range + currency (ISO 4217: INR / USD / AED) — normalized across both platforms; is_salary_hidden flag tells you when salary was withheld
  • AmbitionBox employer rating + review count + URL — unique employer quality signal not available in standard job scrapes
  • company_tags — Private / Public / Foreign MNC / Forbes Global 2000 / B2B classification from Naukri's company graph
  • company_benefits — employee-reported perks with how many employees reported each one (e.g. Free meal x113, Health insurance x91)
  • company_similar_companies — up to 3 competitors with ratings, review counts, and logos; useful for building prospect lists
  • experience range (min/max years + experience_text) — structured from Naukri's experience badge
  • skills, employment_type, walk-in flag, expired flag — full job-level metadata for filtering and routing

Who Should Use This

Recruitment agencies and HR tech platforms covering the Indian and Gulf markets need AmbitionBox ratings and company tags to help candidates evaluate employers — data that Naukri's own export feature does not include. Sales teams targeting Indian enterprises use company_tags (Forbes Global 2000, Foreign MNC) and company_similar_companies to build segmented prospect lists directly from job data. Compensation benchmarking tools value the ISO 4217 currency normalization across INR, USD, and AED, which makes cross-platform salary analysis possible without manual currency mapping. Job aggregators serving the India and MENA region get a deduplicated, 45-field feed from a single actor call across both Naukri and NaukriGulf.
