TED Tenders Scraper: EU Public Procurement Notices

Cost math

Priced at $0.001 per notice delivered ($1.00 per 1,000). The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Input

Put words or phrases in keywords, one search each; a notice found by several keywords is delivered once. countries takes ISO codes of the buyer country such as DE, FR or AT. cpvCodes takes CPV codes such as 72000000 (IT services); a code also matches the more specific codes below it. noticeTypes is contract, award, prior-information or modification. publishedFrom and publishedTo limit the publication date. activeOnly keeps only notices whose submission deadline has not passed. language (default EN) sets the language of title and description. expertQuery adds a condition in TED expert search syntax. maxResults (default 100) caps the notices of the run. Results come newest first. An empty input runs a small sample search.
