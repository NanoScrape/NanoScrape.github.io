Indeed Scraper: 60+ Countries, No Login

Scrape Indeed job listings across 60+ countries from indeed.com, indeed.de, indeed.co.uk, indeed.fr and more. Returns 50+ fields per job including title, company, location, salary range with currency, employment type, remote option, full description in text and HTML, company benefits, posted date, expiry date and apply URL. What you can scrape

  • Job listings: title, company, location, country, employment_type, remote_option, posted_at, expires_at
  • Salary intelligence: salary_min, salary_max, salary_currency, salary_period, salary_text
  • Full job content: description_full, description_html, description_snippet, requirements, company_benefits
  • Company enrichment (Full mode): company rating, CEO, industry, revenue, headquarters, happiness score, skills
  • Trust signals: top_listing flag, job_status, company_logo_url, source_url, apply_url

Primary use cases

  • Talent market mapping. Pull 60+ countries of Indeed search data weekly to map hiring volume by city, role and seniority
  • Sales lead generation. Detect companies actively hiring (a strong growth signal) and feed the list into your CRM
  • Salary benchmarking. Aggregate salary_min and salary_max across regions to size offers vs. market
  • ATS aggregator backfill. Use Full mode to ingest description_html plus company enrichment into your own job board
