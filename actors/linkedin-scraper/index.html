Who Should Use This

Recruiters and talent sourcers get hiring-manager contact details, applicant counts, and company funding signals in one call, removing the need for manual LinkedIn browsing. How much does the linkedin-scraper cost?

What fields does it return?

Is scraping this site legal?

Scraping publicly available data is generally permitted in most jurisdictions, but GDPR applies to any personal data you collect. Review your local rules and consult a lawyer for commercial use.

Do I need an API key from the source platform?

No. This actor reads public pages over HTTP with datacenter proxies, switching to residential proxies only when a request is blocked. No API key, no OAuth, no rate-limit management on your side.

How fresh is the data?
