What You Get

  • title / address / city / district — full location details down to neighborhood level
  • price / price_numeric — listing price in EUR as both display string and machine-readable number
  • rooms / room_configuration — Finnish room layout notation (e.g. '2h, k, kph') plus numeric room count
  • area_sqm — living area in square meters for price-per-sqm calculations
  • building_type — apartment, row house, detached house, terraced house, and more
  • building_year / floor / floor_count — property age and position data
  • image_url — main listing photo for visual previews in the dataset viewer
  • broker_name / realtor_name / source_url — agent details and canonical link back to Oikotie.fi

Who Should Use This

Finnish proptech startups and real estate analytics firms use Oikotie.fi data as the primary market signal for the Finnish residential market because Oikotie.fi is the Sanoma Group portal that competes directly with ETUOVI.com and is one of the two dominant listing platforms in Finland. Property investors and portfolio managers track price-per-sqm trends by city and district across buy listings, comparing Helsinki, Tampere, Turku, and Oulu over time. Relocation services and expat platforms ingest rent listings for their clients moving to Finland, filtering by city and room count. Real estate data brokers pair Oikotie.fi scrapes with Tori.fi classifieds data (also available as a separate actor) to cover both new agency listings and private seller ads in a single Finnish data pipeline.
