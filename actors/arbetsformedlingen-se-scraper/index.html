Arbetsformedlingen Scraper: Sweden's Public Employment Service, 28 Fields

Scrape job listings from Arbetsförmedlingen, Sweden's official public employment service, via the free JobTech API. Returns 28 fields per job: title, full description, company_name, company_logo, location, municipality, region, country, employment_type, working_hours, scope_of_work_min and scope_of_work_max, salary_description, occupation, occupation_field, experience_required, number_of_vacancies, employer_name and employer_url. Covers every Swedish municipality from Stockholm to Kiruna.

What you can scrape

  • Listing core: title, description, company_name, location, municipality, region (län)
  • Swedish occupation taxonomy: occupation, occupation_field, experience_required
  • Working time: working_hours, scope_of_work_min, scope_of_work_max, employment_type
  • Hiring volume: number_of_vacancies (often shows when employers are hiring 5+ at once)
  • Employer data: company_logo, employer_name, employer_url, salary_description

Primary use cases

  • Sweden hiring intelligence. Official government source, free under JobTech, ideal for academic and policy research
  • Bulk-hire detection. Filter number_of_vacancies > 5 to find Swedish employers ramping up teams
  • Municipality-level demand. Aggregate by municipality to track hiring activity in Stockholm vs Göteborg vs Malmö
  • Cross-Nordic backfill. Combine with the Jobindex.dk scraper to cover all Danish and Swedish public listings in one workflow
