What you get

  • Verdict: status (valid / invalid / risky / unknown), confidence (0-100), is_deliverable
  • Syntax checks: is_valid_format, is_ascii_only, local_part, domain
  • MX layer: mx_found, mx_provider (Google, Microsoft 365, Zoho, etc.), mx_records[]
  • SMTP layer: smtp_check, smtp_response, is_catch_all
  • Risk flags: is_disposable, is_role_based (info@, sales@), is_free_provider (gmail, yahoo)

Primary use cases

  • Cold email hygiene. Clean a 50,000-row prospect list before launching an outbound sequence to protect sender reputation
  • Lead-list cleansing. Strip disposable, role-based, and undeliverable addresses from imported lists
  • Signup form protection. Validate emails server-side before creating accounts to cut bot signups
  • CRM deliverability cleanup. Re-verify a CRM quarterly to flag stale addresses before they bounce
  • Sales tool quality control. Verify emails returned by other tools before adding them to sequences
