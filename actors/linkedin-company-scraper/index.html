LinkedIn Company Scraper: Public Company Data Without a Login

Extract public LinkedIn company data programmatically without a login, browser, or LinkedIn API key. Get employee count, industry, headquarters, description, specialties, follower count, top employees, and recent company posts for any LinkedIn company profile. Input a list of LinkedIn URLs or company slugs and get structured data back. Pay-per-result at $5 per 1,000 companies.

What You Get

  • employee_count / employee_count_range — exact headcount and LinkedIn range band (e.g. '1,001-5,000'); the most requested enrichment field for ICP scoring
  • industry / company_type / founded — classification fields for segmentation and prioritization
  • headquarters — city, country, postal code, and full formatted address
  • description / specialties — full company description and up to 10 specialty tags
  • follower_count — LinkedIn audience size as a brand-strength signal
  • employees — optional array of employee profiles filtered by title keywords (HR, CEO, CTO, Recruiter, etc.) with name and LinkedIn URL
  • posts — up to 5 recent company updates from the LinkedIn feed
  • Domain validation — cross-checks the company website domain so mismatched profiles are flagged and excluded automatically

Who Should Use This

CRM enrichment teams bulk-enrich prospect lists with headcount, industry, and HQ location from LinkedIn at a fraction of the cost of dedicated data providers. Sales teams doing ICP scoring filter a prospect list by company size range or industry vertical before outreach, removing the manual lookup step entirely. Market researchers map competitive landscapes by pulling all companies in a niche along with their specialties and follower counts. HR and people teams track headcount changes over time as a hiring or growth signal. The optional includeEmployees mode adds name and LinkedIn URL for key contacts filtered by job title, making this actor a two-in-one company enrichment and contact-discovery tool.
