SEPE Spain Jobs Scraper (Empleate.gob.es)

Extract job listings from Spain's national public employment service (SEPE) via empleate.gob.es (source platform). Returns title, company, location, province, autonomous community, employment type, salary, GPS coordinates, vacancies, seniority level, education/experience requirements, full description and contact details. Covers all 17 comunidades autonomas plus Ceuta and Melilla.

Who Should Use This

Spanish HR tech platforms and labour-market research institutions need SEPE data because it is the only job source in Spain that carries CNO occupation codes, GPS workplace coordinates, and disability-accessible flags as structured first-class fields — data that private job boards do not publish. Disability employment services and foundations use the disability_accessible flag and vacancies count to surface verified government-listed inclusive positions across all regions. Regional economic development agencies use community_code and GPS coordinates to build employment heatmaps at province and municipality level. EU/EURES mobility programs benefit from SEPE's unique coverage of public employment office listings that never appear on commercial boards like InfoJobs or Tecnoempleo. Note: SEPE-direct postings often have a null company_name by design; the hiring entity is described in plain text within description.
