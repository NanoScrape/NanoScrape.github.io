SEPE Spain Jobs Scraper: National Employment Service Data with GPS and Occupation Codes

Scrape job listings from empleate.gob.es, Spain's official national public employment portal (SEPE). Get title, company, autonomous community, province, CNO occupation code, GPS coordinates, disability flag, vacancies count, salary, and full description. What You Get

  • region / community_code — one of Spain's 19 administrative regions (17 comunidades + Ceuta + Melilla), machine-readable as community_code
  • province / location — Spanish province and city for sub-regional geo-routing
  • latitude / longitude — GPS coordinates of the workplace for map-based job search features
  • cno_code — Spanish National Occupation Classification code (Clasificacion Nacional de Ocupaciones) for sector-level analytics
  • disability_accessible / vacancies — SEPE disability flag and number of open positions for this listing
  • employment_type / workplace_type — permanent / fixed-term / commission-contract + on-site / remote normalized from SEPE's own classification
  • description / description_full / description_html / description_md — four description formats: snippet, plain text, raw HTML, and Markdown
  • source_origin — identifies whether the listing came from a SEPE partner (TECNO_EMPLEO, InfoJobs, HacesFalta) or directly from a PUP office

Who Should Use This

Spanish HR tech platforms and labour-market research institutions need SEPE data because it is the only job source in Spain that carries CNO occupation codes, GPS workplace coordinates, and disability-accessible flags as structured first-class fields — data that private job boards do not publish. Disability employment services and foundations use the disability_accessible flag and vacancies count to surface verified government-listed inclusive positions across all regions. Regional economic development agencies use community_code and GPS coordinates to build employment heatmaps at province and municipality level. EU/EURES mobility programs benefit from SEPE's unique coverage of public employment office listings that never appear on commercial boards like InfoJobs or Tecnoempleo. Note: SEPE-direct postings often have a null company_name by design; the hiring entity is described in plain text within description.
