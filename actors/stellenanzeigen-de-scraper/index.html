Priced at $0.0075 per job detail result. 10 job detail results ≈ $0.07, 100 ≈ $0.75, 1,000 ≈ $7.50. The first runs land inside Apify's $5/mo free-tier credit — pay only for what you extract, no monthly subscription.

Who Should Use This

German job aggregators and labour analytics platforms add Stellenanzeigen.de to their pipeline because it sits in Germany's Top 3 by listing volume alongside StepStone and Monster, meaning it captures employers and roles not mirrored on the other two. Contact prospecting teams extract contact_email and contact_phone from job descriptions to build recruiter outreach lists without a separate email-scraping step. Bundesland-level market researchers use the auto-detected state code to produce per-Bundesland hiring trend reports for all 16 German states without any manual location tagging. The ultra-low 128 MB footprint and HTTP-only approach make it one of the cheapest German job scrapers to run at scale: no browser instance, no Chromium memory overhead.
