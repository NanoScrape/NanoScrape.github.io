Who Should Use This

German job aggregators and labour analytics platforms add Stellenanzeigen.de to their pipeline because it sits in Germany's Top 3 by listing volume alongside StepStone and Monster, meaning it captures employers and roles not mirrored on the other two. Contact prospecting teams extract contact_email and contact_phone from job descriptions to build recruiter outreach lists without a separate email-scraping step. Bundesland-level market researchers use the auto-detected state code to produce per-Bundesland hiring trend reports for all 16 German states without any manual location tagging.
