What you get

  • Location: country_iso, country_name, region, city, postal_code, latitude, longitude
  • Network: asn, asn_org, isp, connection_type, network, hostname
  • Time: timezone, utc_offset, currency_code
  • Risk signals: is_proxy, is_vpn, is_tor, is_hosting, is_anonymous
  • Metadata: ip_version, accuracy_radius_km, scraped_at

Primary use cases

  • Log analysis. Geo-enrich web server access logs to map global traffic patterns
  • Fraud scoring. Flag VPN, Tor, and hosting-provider IPs in signup and checkout flows
  • Security audits. Map source countries of failed logins, brute-force attempts, and 4xx spikes
  • Compliance geo-fencing. Verify users match regional licensing or sanctioned-country rules
  • Marketing analytics. Break down traffic by city and ASN to identify B2B prospects
