What you get

  • Traffic volume: monthly_visits, monthly_unique_visitors, avg_visit_duration_sec, pages_per_visit, bounce_rate
  • Traffic sources: direct_pct, search_pct, social_pct, referral_pct, mail_pct, paid_pct
  • Keywords: top_organic_keywords[], top_paid_keywords[], keyword traffic share
  • Rankings: global_rank, country_rank, category, category_rank
  • Geo + ecosystem: top_countries[], top_referrers[], top_destinations[], competitors[]

Primary use cases

  • Competitive intelligence. Benchmark your monthly visits, keyword share, and traffic mix against named competitors
  • Market sizing. Estimate addressable category traffic by summing top-N players in a vertical
  • Content strategy. Reverse-engineer which keywords drive a competitor's organic traffic before planning your own content
  • ABM scoring. Rank target accounts by monthly_visits, paid_share, and tech stack before outbound
  • Acquisition due diligence. Verify a target's claimed traffic and source mix from an independent vantage point
