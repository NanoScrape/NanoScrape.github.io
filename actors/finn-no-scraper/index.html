Who Should Use This

This actor suits Norwegian job aggregators, classifieds-market researchers, and Scandinavian e-commerce analysts. Finn.no is Norway's dominant classifieds and job platform (Schibsted group) with combined coverage of jobs, real estate, cars, and second-hand goods in a single search interface. Job aggregators covering the Norwegian market use this as their primary source. Price-comparison and used-goods intelligence platforms use the marketplace vertical for NOK pricing data. The multi-vertical capability -- one actor for jobs, cars, and marketplaces -- avoids the need to maintain separate scrapers for what is effectively one platform.
