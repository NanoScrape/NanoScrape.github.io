What we build

Every actor on nanoscrape.com is a small, focused, production-grade scraper you can run on demand from the Apify cloud. Point it at a URL or search query, get structured JSON back, pay only for what you extract. No monthly subscription. No API keys to manage on the source platforms.

Our catalogue is grouped into ten hubs:

  • Job Scrapers — national job boards + individual company career pages (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS, Taleo, SmartRecruiters)
  • Business Directories — Google Maps, Yelp, Cylex, Kompass, Yellow Pages, Europages
  • Marketplaces — classifieds and cars (AutoScout24, Mobile.de, Subito.it)
  • Social Media — Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, Reddit, YouTube
  • Ecommerce — Amazon, eBay, AliExpress, Shopify storefronts
  • Real Estate — ImmoScout24, Idealista, Zillow, Homegate
  • Reviews — Trustpilot, Yelp reviews, Google reviews
  • Enrichment — contact extraction, LLM-augmented profiles
  • Web Tools — PDF, Wikipedia, link checking, generic utilities
  • Search & News — Google Search, news feeds

How we build

Most of our actors are HTTP-only Go programs — cheap to run, fast, no browser overhead. When a source requires JavaScript rendering or ships strict bot-protection stacks, we deploy a browser-tier variant and keep iterating toward an HTTP-only version as soon as the underlying protection is understood.

Reliability engineering is where a lot of our time goes: TLS + HTTP/2 fingerprint alignment, careful session and header pacing, residential proxy pools where the source demands them. We publish reference notes and templates on our GitHub organization.

How we're different

  • Pay per result, not per subscription. Every actor is priced in the openrange of $0.001–$0.02 per structured record. Apify credits your account with $5/month for free — enough for meaningful testing on any scraper before you commit a cent.
  • Country depth over global breadth. We put the effort into the local platforms competitors ignore (jobs.ch, Arbeit.Swiss, HH.ru, Trudvsem.ru, Kompass, InfoJobs, Homegate, ImmobilienScout24), not just LinkedIn and Google.
  • Deploy-ready outputs. Every actor emits a stable JSON schema with a public field catalog, PII-scrubbed example, and n8n/Zapier/Make/MCP integration snippets on its own landing page.
  • No lock-in. Runs happen in the customer's Apify account. Datasets belong to the customer. Actors are open to inspection on Apify's public store.
