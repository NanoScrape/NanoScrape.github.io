How to find companies hiring in Germany with StepStone

You want to know which companies in a German city are hiring for a given role, for example sales in München, so you can offer them candidates, software or services? The StepStone Scraper collects the ads, and a short Google Sheets pivot turns them into one row per company with the number of open roles, the job titles, company size, industry and website. No code needed.

Why group by company

A StepStone search returns one row per job ad. For a lead list you want one row per company: who is hiring for this role in this city, and how many roles are open. A company with five open sales positions is a different prospect than one with a single ad. The Actor gives you the ads; a short pivot in Google Sheets turns them into the company list. The general StepStone export tutorial covers the plain job export and all filters. This page is only about the company view.

STEP 1

Open the StepStone Actor

  1. Open the StepStone Scraper on Apify. The Actor page on NanoScrape lists every output field.
  2. Click Try for free and sign up with Google or email. Apify's free plan includes monthly platform credit, enough for a first test.
  3. You land on the Actor's Input tab. Switch to the JSON view to paste the input below.
STEP 2

Paste this input

Example: all sales roles (“Vertrieb”) in and around München, posted in the last 30 days. Replace the keyword and city with yours.

{
  "searchQueries": [
    "Vertrieb"
  ],
  "location": "München",
  "radius": 25,
  "postedWithinDays": 30,
  "sortBy": "newest",
  "titleFilter": "Vertrieb",
  "maxResultsPerQuery": 100,
  "includeJobDetails": true
}
  • searchQueries: the role, in German as the ads are written. Add synonyms, for example “Vertriebsmitarbeiter” or “Account Manager”; duplicates are removed by job ID.
  • location and radius: city and radius in km (default 25).
  • postedWithinDays: 30 keeps the list to companies hiring now, not last year.
  • titleFilter: keeps only ads whose title contains the term, so a company that merely mentions sales in the text does not end up in your list.
  • includeJobDetails: keep it true. The company profile fields used below (website, size, industry, job count) come from the detail page.
  • maxResultsPerQuery: start at 20 for a test, then raise it.
Narrow title filter, broad keyword? The Actor scans up to 10 result pages per query before it applies the title filter. If you filter narrowly, raise maxPagesFetched so enough pages are read.
STEP 3

Run it and read the output

Click Save and Run. When the run ends, open the Dataset tab. Each row is one ad. This is a real item from the Actor (a Dortmund IT ad, shortened to the fields that matter for this task; the long description, requirements and benefits fields are left out):

{
  "id": "14692006",
  "title": "Werkstudent C# Softwareentwicklung (m/w/d)",
  "company": "Materna Information & Communications SE",
  "location": "Dortmund",
  "state": "Nordrhein-Westfalen",
  "employment_type": "full-time",
  "posted_at": "2026-11-12T10:03:08.597Z",
  "source_url": "https://www.stepstone.de/stellenangebote--Werkstudent-C-Softwareentwicklung-m-w-d-Dortmund-Materna-Information-Communications-SE--14692006-inline.htm...",
  "company_url": "https://www.stepstone.de/cmp/de/Materna-Information-Communications-SE-4261/jobs.html",
  "company_website": "https://www.materna.de/",
  "company_job_count": 15,
  "company_size": "1000+ Mitarbeiter",
  "industry": "IT, IT-Softwareentwicklung",
  "company_address_city": "Dortmund",
  "search_query": "Software Engineer",
  "scraped_at": "2026-11-12T10:43:51Z"
}

The fields you need for a company list:

  • company: the grouping key.
  • company_url: the company's profile on StepStone, with all of its ads.
  • company_website: the company's own site, a good starting point for outreach. It is not filled for every company, so expect gaps.
  • company_job_count: how many jobs the company has open on StepStone in total, not only in your city.
  • company_size and industry: to pick the segment you can serve.
  • title, location, posted_at, source_url: the evidence for your first message (“I saw your ad for ...”).

This tutorial does not use any contact-person fields. Reach companies through their public channels, and check the data-protection rules for your use before you store personal data.

STEP 4

Turn the ads into a company list

  1. In the Dataset tab click Export, choose CSV and select the fields company, title, location, company_url, company_website, company_job_count, company_size, industry, posted_at, source_url.
  2. Import the file into Google Sheets (File → Import) as a new sheet called jobs. The columns are A to J in the order above.
  3. On a new sheet companies, put this in A2 to list each company once:
=SORT(UNIQUE(jobs!A2:A))
  1. In B2, count the open ads in your search for that company, and drag down:
=COUNTIF(jobs!A:A, A2)
  1. In C2, list the job titles so you see what they are hiring for:
=TEXTJOIN(" | ", TRUE, FILTER(jobs!B:B, jobs!A:A = A2))
  1. Add the website with =XLOOKUP(A2, jobs!A:A, jobs!E:E) and do the same for company size (column G). Then sort by column B, largest first.

You now have one row per company with the number of open roles and the titles, ready to go through. Two checks before you use the list. First, staffing and recruiting firms advertise on StepStone too, and this Actor has no filter to hide them, so delete those rows by name if you only want companies hiring for themselves. Second, the list covers companies that advertise on StepStone, not every company that is hiring.

Keep it fresh with a schedule

In the Apify Console, open Schedules and run the same input once a week, with sortBy set to newest. Paste each new export under the old one and use id to drop ads you already have. New companies appearing in the list are the ones that just started hiring.

Frequently asked questions

Does this list every company hiring in a city?

No. It lists the companies that advertise on StepStone for your search term, location and time window. Companies that hire only through their own site or other boards are not in it.

Can I hide recruitment agencies?

This Actor has no agency filter. Agencies show up under their own name, so remove those rows in the sheet, for example by a keyword list in a helper column.

Why is company_website empty for some companies?

The website comes from the company profile and is not published for every company. Use company_url, the StepStone profile, or a search for the company name to find it.

How much does it cost to build a list for one city?

Pay per event: $0.003 per job ad, plus $0.005 when the ad includes full details. With details on, 100 ads are about $0.80. You are charged per ad, and one company can have several ads.
