A short Google Sheets step turns it into your prospect list. No code needed.

Why a category page works better than a keyword

FirmenABC groups companies by trade and by state or city. A category URL such as IT services in Wien lists exactly the companies in that trade and place, which is the list a B2B prospecting project starts from. The keyword search is different: it covers all of Austria and ignores the location field, so it is the wrong tool for a city list. This page builds a Vienna IT-company list from a category URL. The German tutorial shows a second example, a trade list for Graz with phone and email.

STEP 1

Open the Actor

  1. Open the FirmenABC Scraper on Apify. The Actor page on NanoScrape lists every output field.
  2. Click Try for free and sign up with Google or email. Apify's free plan includes monthly platform credit, enough for a first test.
  3. You land on the Actor's Input tab. Switch to the JSON view to paste the input below.
STEP 2

Find the category URL and paste the input

On firmenabc.at open the state or city and pick the trade. Copy the address of the list page. Example: all companies in the category IT services (IT-Dienstleistungen) in Wien.

{
  "searchUrls": [
    "https://www.firmenabc.at/firmen/wien/it-dienstleistungen_CXq"
  ],
  "maxResults": 100,
  "includeDetails": true
}
  • searchUrls: one or more list pages. Other real examples are all companies in Burgenland (https://www.firmenabc.at/firmen/bgld) or restaurants in Steiermark (https://www.firmenabc.at/firmen/stmk/restaurant_CJg). State slugs: wien, noe, ooe, stmk, sbg, tirol, ktn, vbg, bgld.
  • maxResults: how many companies to collect. Start at 10 for a test.
  • includeDetails: true (the default) opens each company page for website, email, phone, description, opening hours and more. With false you only get the list data.
Keyword search is Austria-wide. The searchQuery field returns companies from all of Austria and ignores location. For a city or state, always use a list URL in searchUrls.
STEP 3

Run it and read the output

Click Save and Run, then open the Dataset tab. Each row is one company. This is the sample item from the Actor's README (a delicatessen in St. Poelten, shown only to illustrate the structure), shortened to the company fields used here:

{
  "id": "BBSNi",
  "name": "Famos Delikatessen",
  "website": "https://www.famos-delikatessen.at",
  "address": "Domgasse 10",
  "city": "St. Poelten",
  "postal_code": "3100",
  "country_code": "AT",
  "latitude": 48.2081,
  "longitude": 15.6267,
  "categories": ["Delikatessengeschaeft / Feinkost in Sankt Poelten (Stadt)"],
  "rating": 5.0,
  "review_count": 1,
  "employee_count": "6",
  "founding_year": 1982,
  "source_url": "https://www.firmenabc.at/famos-delikatessen_BBSNi",
  "source_platform": "firmenabc.at",
  "scraped_at": "2026-03-26T10:00:00Z"
}

The fields for a B2B list:

  • name, website, address, postal_code and city: the company and where it sits. Use postal_code to cut a city list by district.
  • categories: the trades the company is listed under.
  • employee_count and founding_year: company size and age, read from the company portrait. They are empty when the portrait does not state them.
  • rating and review_count: public ratings.
  • latitude and longitude: to put the companies on a map.
  • source_url: the company page on FirmenABC. id is the stable key for dropping duplicates.

The company page can also carry company phone, email and a block of register contacts. This tutorial uses company data only and leaves the person fields out. Check data-protection and unfair-competition rules for your use before you contact anyone.

STEP 4

Turn the export into a prospect list

  1. In the Dataset tab click Export, choose CSV and select name, website, address, postal_code, city, categories, employee_count, founding_year, rating and source_url.
  2. Import the file into Google Sheets (File → Import).
  3. Sort by employee_count to start with the larger companies, or filter founding_year for young firms. Companies without a website can be checked by hand or dropped.
  4. Repeat the run with a second category URL, for example another state, and paste the new rows below. Use id to remove duplicates.

The list shows companies that FirmenABC lists, not every company in the trade.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get companies of one trade in one city?

Yes. Open the trade and city on firmenabc.at, copy the list page URL and paste it into searchUrls. The keyword search is Austria-wide and ignores the location field.

Which states can I use?

State pages use the slugs wien, noe, ooe, stmk, sbg, tirol, ktn, vbg and bgld, for example firmenabc.at/firmen/wien.

Why are employee_count or website empty for some companies?

They are only filled when the company page states them. Many companies have no employee paragraph or no website listed.

Does the list contain every company in the trade?

No. It contains the companies that FirmenABC lists for that category and place.

Pay per result: $5.00 per 1,000 business details. 100 companies with details are $0.50.
