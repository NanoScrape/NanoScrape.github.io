The fragmentation problem: 300+ job platforms, no unified feed

By the NanoScrape team - Published 2026-09-02 - Updated 2026-09-02

Every recruiter, talent intelligence team, and job aggregator hits the same wall eventually: the jobs data you need is not in one place. It never has been. The global job market is fragmented across hundreds of platforms, and the fragmentation runs three layers deep.

Layer 1: Aggregators cover only what employers choose to post

LinkedIn and Indeed are the most-cited sources for job data, and for good reason: between them they index tens of millions of active listings. But both are opt-in platforms. Employers post there deliberately, often paying per-click or per-application. Thousands of companies, particularly mid-size enterprises, government bodies, and specialist employers, post exclusively on their own careers site or on niche vertical boards. Those roles never appear on LinkedIn or Indeed.

Layer 2: Careers sites are not standardized

The 500 largest US companies alone run 24 different Applicant Tracking Systems. Workday, Phenom, Oracle Recruiting, iCIMS, TalentBrew, SuccessFactors, Taleo, Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, Avature, Brassring, Paradox, Eightfold, Beamery, Jobvite, BambooHR, PageUp, UKG, ADP: each has a different API shape, different field names, different pagination, and different anti-bot posture. Scraping even one ATS reliably is a month of engineering. Scraping all of them is a product.

Layer 3: Geography adds a multiplier

Job search is local. A candidate in Warsaw uses Pracuj.pl. A hiring manager in Dublin posts on IrishJobs.ie. A public-sector employer in France lists on HelloWork. A tech company in Poland mandates salary on NoFluffJobs. None of those surfaces appear in a US-centric jobs scraper. Truly global coverage means 25+ country-specific platforms on top of the cross-border ones.

The result: any team trying to build a jobs data pipeline from scratch has to solve ATS fragmentation, custom-site extraction, JS rendering, anti-bot, and geography, all at once, in every market they care about. This guide shows how the NanoScrape catalog handles all of it.

The 3-tier stack and the Job Feed orchestrator

Before diving into the country and vertical catalog, here is the technical architecture that makes complete coverage possible. Every NanoScrape jobs scraper fits into one of three tiers based on how the target site serves its data. The Job Feed orchestrator sits above all three and handles routing, retries, deduplication, and schema unification automatically.

  • Tier 1 - Career Site Jobs Scraper (santamaria-automations/career-site-jobs-scraper): Talks directly to the JSON APIs of 24+ Applicant Tracking Systems (Workday, Phenom, Oracle Recruiting, iCIMS, TalentBrew, Eightfold, Brassring, Avature, Taleo modern, Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, ADP, and more). HTTP-only, no browser, fast and cheap at $1 per 1,000 jobs.
  • Tier 2 - Website Job Extractor (santamaria-automations/website-job-extractor): LLM-powered HTTP scraper for any careers page with no supported ATS API. Auto-discovers the careers page, cleans the HTML, sends it to Gemini (or Groq/OpenRouter), and returns validated job records with confidence scores. Also auto-detects ATS platforms on the way in.
  • Tier 3 - Website Job Extractor Browser (santamaria-automations/website-job-extractor-browser): Playwright-powered variant for sites that render jobs exclusively in JavaScript (React, Vue, Angular SPAs). Automatically spawned by Tier 2 when JS rendering is detected.
  • Job Feed orchestrator (santamaria-automations/job-feed) - private beta: Give it a list of company URLs or platform URLs. It detects each site's ATS, fans out to the right tier actor, runs all three in parallel, deduplicates on job_url, unifies the schema across tiers, and emits one clean dataset.
For the full technical playbook, input examples, output schemas, mapper script, cost math, and n8n automation, see the companion tutorial: How to Scrape Every Fortune 500 Job Listing. This guide focuses on the catalog: which actors cover which platforms, in which countries and verticals.

Jobs scraper query strategy: how our catalog achieves 100% coverage attempted

Someone searching for a "jobs scraper" has one of four underlying needs. Understanding which one they have determines which tool in the stack to reach for.

Need 1: Scrape a known job board or platform

The searcher knows the platform - Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, LinkedIn, Totaljobs, InfoJobs, etc. - and wants a reliable, maintained scraper for it. This is the catalog use case: find the right actor in the country and vertical tables above, run it against the platform, get structured output. Every actor is purpose-built for its platform with maintained selectors, anti-bot handling, and fleet-standard output fields.

Need 2: Scrape a company's careers site (ATS-native)

The searcher wants jobs from a specific company, and that company runs Workday, Phenom, Oracle Recruiting, iCIMS, or one of 20+ other supported ATS platforms. The right tool is the Career Site Jobs Scraper: it talks to the ATS's JSON API directly, returning structured data at $1 per 1,000 jobs with no LLM cost and no browser overhead.

Need 3: Scrape any careers site with no known ATS

The searcher has a list of company URLs, some on supported ATS platforms, some with custom-built careers sites. For the custom sites, there is no stable HTML parser: every site is structured differently. The right tool is the Website Job Extractor (Tier 2). When it detects a supported ATS on the way in, it emits the ATS URL so you can switch to Tier 1 on the next run and cut costs by 10x.

Need 4: Unified multi-source jobs feed at scale

The searcher wants a single deduplicated, normalized feed of jobs from dozens of sources refreshed daily. This is the Job Feed orchestrator use case. Give it a list of URLs, and it routes each one to the correct tier actor, runs them in parallel, deduplicates on job_url, unifies the schema across tiers, and emits one clean dataset per run. Currently in private beta.

The coverage math: ATS-native (Tier 1) covers companies running supported ATS platforms - historically 58%+ of large enterprises. LLM HTTP extraction (Tier 2) covers custom careers sites and smaller companies - another 27%+. Browser fallback (Tier 3) covers JS-only rendered sites - another 10%+. Across the platform catalog (job boards, not just company careers sites), the 62 actors in this guide cover the dominant surfaces for 25 countries - representing the majority of publicly accessible job postings worldwide.

FAQ

Which jobs scraper should I use if I don't know which platform a company uses?

Start with the Website Job Extractor with skipAtsExtraction: false. It auto-discovers the careers page and, if it detects a supported ATS platform, returns the ATS URL so you can switch to the cheaper Career Site Jobs Scraper on the next run.

Do these job scrapers work on LinkedIn and Indeed?

Yes: LinkedIn (linkedin-scraper) uses the guest-view API which requires no login, and Indeed (indeed-scraper) is HTTP-only via Camoufox residential. Both are in the Global aggregators section above. For the majority of job data use cases, however, our country and vertical actors cover many roles LinkedIn and Indeed never index (particularly company ATS pages and government boards).

How do I combine output from multiple job scrapers into one dataset?

Two options. Manual: use the mapper script from the Fortune 500 tutorial - a 50-line Python normalizer. Automated: use the Job Feed orchestrator (private beta), which handles routing, normalization, and deduplication internally.

Can I scrape jobs in multiple countries at once?

Yes. Some actors are inherently multi-country: welcome-to-the-jungle-scraper covers FR/UK/DE/ES/CZ; seek-scraper covers 8 APAC countries; computrabajo-scraper covers 19 LATAM countries; hh-ru-scraper covers RU/KZ/UZ/BY. You can also run multiple single-country actors in parallel on Apify and merge datasets - the Job Feed orchestrator does this automatically.

Is there a free way to test these scrapers before committing?

Yes. Every actor on Apify has a free trial built in via Apify's $5/month free plan. At $1 per 1,000 jobs on the ATS scraper, $5 runs about 5,000 jobs.
