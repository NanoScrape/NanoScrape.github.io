Why filter for part-time at the source

Many Swiss job ads state a workload, written as 60%, 80 to 100% or similar. The JobScout24.ch Actor reads the type into employment_type and the range into workload_min and workload_max, and it filters by employmentType and region before the ads are saved. That gives you one clean table of part-time jobs for one region instead of a long list to read by hand. For the plain export of JobScout24 listings, see the German guide JobScout24 Stellenanzeigen exportieren.

STEP 1

Open the JobScout24 Actor

  1. Open the JobScout24.ch Scraper on Apify. The Actor page on NanoScrape lists every output field.
  2. Click Try for free and sign up with Google or email. Apify's free plan includes monthly platform credit, enough for a first test.
  3. You land on the Actor's Input tab. Switch to the JSON view to paste the input below.
STEP 2

Paste this input

Example: part-time administration jobs (“Sachbearbeiter”) in the Zurich region, posted in the last 14 days, newest first. Replace the keyword with your job title.

{
  "searchQueries": [
    "Sachbearbeiter"
  ],
  "region": "zurich",
  "employmentType": "part-time",
  "postedWithinDays": 14,
  "sortBy": "newest",
  "maxResultsPerQuery": 100,
  "includeJobDetails": true
}
  • searchQueries: the job title, in German as the ads are written. Add synonyms such as “Sachbearbeiterin”; duplicates across keywords are removed.
  • region: zurich here. Other values are bern, basel, aargau, ostschweiz, zentralschweiz, romandie, tessin, graubunden, wallis and remote. Leave it empty to search all of Switzerland.
  • employmentType: part-time. The other values are full-time, temporary, apprenticeship and internship.
  • postedWithinDays: 14 keeps the list to recent ads. The site shows ages in whole days, so 1 keeps today and yesterday.
  • sortBy: newest puts the latest ads first.
  • maxResultsPerQuery: start at 20 for a test, then raise it.
  • includeJobDetails: keep it true for the full description, apply link and company data. Turn it off for a faster list with listing data only.
Narrow by field of work too. Add "category": "healthcare" (or it, finance, sales, education and others) to stay inside one field, and titleFilter to keep only titles that contain your words.

The fields you need for a part-time search:

  • title and company: what the job is and who offers it.
  • location and canton: place and canton code, for example ZH.
  • employment_type, workload_min and workload_max: the type and the Pensum range in percent.
  • salary_text: the salary as the ad shows it, when it shows one.
  • posted_at: the posting date.
  • source_url and apply_url: the ad and the way to apply. With details on you also get the full description and the company profile.

With details on, the dataset also has contact fields for some ads. This tutorial does not use them. Reach employers through their public channels, and check the data-protection rules for your use before you store personal data.

STEP 4

Sort the ads in Google Sheets

  1. In the Dataset tab click Export, choose CSV and select title, company, location, workload_min, workload_max, posted_at, source_url.
  2. Import the file into Google Sheets (File → Import). The columns are A to G in the order above.
  3. Keep the ads that fit the share of work you want. For a 60% job, keep rows where the minimum workload (column D) is 60 or less and the maximum (column E) is 60 or more:
=FILTER(A2:G, D2:D<=60, E2:E>=60)
  1. To see which employers advertise the most part-time roles, count ads per company:
=QUERY(A2:G, "select B, count(A) where B is not null group by B order by count(A) desc label count(A) 'Ads'")

The list covers ads on JobScout24.ch only.

Keep it fresh with a schedule

In the Apify Console, open Schedules and run the same input once a week with sortBy set to newest. Use id to drop ads you already have. The postedWithinDays filter keeps each run short.

Frequently asked questions

Does the part-time filter use the workload percentage?

The Actor has an employmentType filter (part-time) and also returns workload_min and workload_max for each ad, so you can narrow further in your sheet, for example to jobs that allow 60%.

Can I search another region than Zurich?

Yes. The region filter takes zurich, bern, basel, aargau, ostschweiz, zentralschweiz, romandie, tessin, graubunden, wallis or remote. Leave it empty to search all of Switzerland, and use the canton column in the output to narrow down.

Is the salary always filled?

No. salary_text holds the salary as the ad shows it, and it stays empty when the ad shows none.

How much does it cost?

The price per job is on the Actor page and can change, so see the current price on the Actor page. You are charged per job saved, and a test with maxResultsPerQuery set to 20 keeps a first run small.
