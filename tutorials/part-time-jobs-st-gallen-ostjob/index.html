How to find part-time jobs in St. Gallen with Ostjob.ch

You want part-time jobs in St. Gallen or elsewhere in Eastern Switzerland, for example nursing at 40 to 60%, in one sheet you can sort and filter? Why search Eastern Switzerland on its own board

Ostjob.ch is a job portal for Eastern Switzerland: St. Gallen, Thurgau, Appenzell, Glarus and Graubünden. If you look for a part-time job, the useful filter is the Pensum: the share of a full-time job, written as 40 to 60% or 80 to 100% in the ad. The Actor reads that range into workload_min and workload_max, so you filter by it directly instead of reading ad by ad. For all fields and filters of the Actor, see the Actor page.

STEP 1

Open the Ostjob Actor

  1. Open the Ostjob.ch Scraper on Apify. The Actor page on NanoScrape lists every output field.
  2. Click Try for free and sign up with Google or email. Apify's free plan includes monthly platform credit, enough for a first test.
  3. You land on the Actor's Input tab. Switch to the JSON view to paste the input below.
STEP 2

Paste this input

Example: part-time nursing jobs (“Pflege”) in St. Gallen with a workload between 40% and 60%, newest first, without recruitment agencies. Replace the keyword and place with yours.

{
  "searchQueries": [
    "Pflege"
  ],
  "location": "St. Gallen",
  "workloadMin": 40,
  "workloadMax": 60,
  "sortBy": "newest",
  "companyFilter": "exclude-hr",
  "maxResultsPerQuery": 100,
  "includeJobDetails": true
}
  • searchQueries: the profession, in German as the ads are written. Add synonyms such as “Pflegefachfrau”; jobs found by several keywords are deduplicated.
  • location: a canton, district or city. The Actor docs give “St. Gallen” and “Thurgau” as examples and work out which kind of place you mean. Leave it empty to search all of Eastern Switzerland.
  • workloadMin and workloadMax: the Pensum range in percent. 40 to 60 is the example the Actor docs give for part-time. Use 80 and 100 for near full-time, or set only one of the two to leave the other side open.
  • companyFilter: exclude-hr removes recruitment agencies, only-hr keeps only them, all is the default.
  • sortBy: newest puts the latest ads first, which is what you want for a weekly check.
  • maxResultsPerQuery: start at 20 for a test, then raise it.
  • includeJobDetails: keep it true for the full description and company data.
One profession, several words? Put all of them into searchQueries. Each keyword is a separate search with its own limit, and the search_query column tells you which keyword found the ad.
STEP 3

Run it and read the output

Click Save and Run. When the run ends, open the Dataset tab. Each row is one ad. The fields that matter for a part-time search:

  • title, company_name: what the job is and who offers it.
  • location and canton: city, ZIP and canton code, for example SG or TG.
  • workload_min and workload_max: the Pensum range of the ad as numbers, so you can sort and filter them.
  • employment_type: full-time or part-time as the board labels it.
  • posted_at and expires_at: when the ad went up and when it ends.
  • job_url and apply_url: the ad and the way to apply.
  • company_website and company_address: the employer's own site and address.
  • remote_option: hybrid or onsite, when the ad says so.

The dataset also has contact fields for some ads. This tutorial does not use them. Reach employers through their public channels, and check the data-protection rules for your use before you store personal data.

STEP 4

Turn the ads into a part-time shortlist

  1. In the Dataset tab click Export, choose CSV and select title, company_name, location, workload_min, workload_max, posted_at, job_url.
  2. Import the file into Google Sheets (File → Import). The columns are A to G in the order above.
  3. Keep the ads that fit the share of work you want. For a 50% job, keep rows where the minimum workload (column D) is 50 or less and the maximum (column E) is 50 or more:
=FILTER(A2:G, D2:D<=50, E2:E>=50)
  1. To see which employers advertise the most part-time roles, count ads per company:
=QUERY(A2:G, "select B, count(A) where B is not null group by B order by count(A) desc label count(A) 'Ads'")

The ads come from Ostjob.ch only, so this is a view of one board for Eastern Switzerland, not of every vacancy in St. Gallen.

Keep it fresh with a schedule

In the Apify Console, open Schedules and run the same input once a week with sortBy set to newest. Use id to drop ads you already have. To see whether a saved ad is still online, run the Actor with directUrls set to the saved job_url values.

Frequently asked questions

Can I search all of Eastern Switzerland instead of one town?

Yes. Leave location empty and the Actor searches all of Eastern Switzerland. Or name a canton such as Thurgau instead of a city.

What do workloadMin and workloadMax mean?

They are the Pensum range in percent. An ad for 40 to 60% has workload_min 40 and workload_max 60. Set both to match the share you want, or set only one to leave the other side open.

Can I hide recruitment agencies?

Yes. Set companyFilter to exclude-hr and the Actor removes recruitment and HR agencies from the results. Use only-hr for the opposite.

How much does it cost?

The price per job is on the Actor page and can change, so see the current price on the Actor page. You are charged per job saved, and a test with maxResultsPerQuery set to 20 keeps a first run small.
